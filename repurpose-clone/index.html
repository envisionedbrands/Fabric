FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Do I need to download and re-upload my content?
No — that's the whole point. Once your accounts are connected, Repurpose.io pulls content directly from the source platform, transforms it, and publishes it to your destinations. Nothing ever touches your hard drive.
Will my TikTok videos have the watermark on other platforms?
No. Videos are pulled without the TikTok watermark, so your Reels, Shorts and other posts look native to each platform — which the algorithms strongly prefer.
Can I review content before it gets published?
Yes. Every workflow can run in fully automatic mode or in review mode, where converted content waits in a queue for your approval before going live.
What happens to my existing back catalog?
You can bulk-import your entire history — every past episode, video or stream — and drip it out on a schedule. Most creators fill months of their content calendar on day one.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no contracts. Cancel in two clicks from your dashboard, and your workflows simply stop running.
