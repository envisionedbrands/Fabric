# Story Bank — Real Stories for Content Creation

All content pulls from these **real stories** — never invented. When Maria-Ines shares a new story in conversation, it gets added here.

**Rule:** These are the only narratives available for building [[story-weave-method]] content. If a story isn't on this list, it's either fabricated or still being developed in private.

---

## Core Origin Stories

### The Venezuela Story
**Category:** Authority, Gravity, Lens
**Used for:** Positioning plays, keynotes, deep content, explaining the philosophy

**The story:**
> "I grew up in Venezuela watching systems fail. Not metaphorically. I'm Venezuelan. I watched an entire country's infrastructure collapse in real time because it was all built around one centralized authority. When that authority cracked, everything cracked."

**What it establishes:**
- Why she's obsessed with codification and distribution
- Why founder-dependency is life-or-death (not just business)
- Authority through lived experience
- The Venezuelan lens as her superpower

**Hook:** [[emotional-hooks#Hook 6 The Venezuelan Lens]]

**Deployment:** Origin story moments, podcast interviews, keynotes, Substack, when establishing why this matters beyond business tactics

### The MCP Moment (Eyes Welling Up)
**Category:** Proof, Transformation, Emotional
**Used for:** Proof of concept, product launches, showing what codification looks like

**The story:**
> "I connected a tool to my business this morning and my eyes welled up. Not because it was beautiful. Not because it was complicated. Because for the first time, I spoke to my business in plain English — and it understood."

**Extended version:**
> "I connected my CRM and said: 'I have a new offer. Help me set it up.' And it already knew. My methodology. My client history. My offer structure. Because it was connected to the architecture I'd already built. The tool didn't do this. I did. The reason it was powerful is because I'd already extracted my methodology, mapped my offers, documented my decision patterns. The AI had something to connect TO."

**What it proves:**
- Codification actually works
- The emotion of the transformation
- Why extraction comes before automation

**Hook:** [[emotional-hooks#Hook 2 The Mirror]]

**Deployment:** Video content, Substack, when explaining what codification feels like, technical articles

### The Time-Rich CEO Flop
**Category:** Vulnerability, Authority, Honesty
**Used for:** Building trust, showing persistence, repositioning failure

**The story:**
> "Last year I launched something called Time-Rich CEO and it flopped. I'm telling you this because everyone shows you the wins. Nobody shows you the moment where the market looks at your work and says 'not yet.'"

While it was dying publicly, I built privately. Async systems, file architecture, systematized my genius. Persisted until the market caught up. That's the story nobody tells — the work between the flop and the rebuild."

**What it establishes:**
- She doesn't follow trends, she waits for them to catch up
- Failure is architecture, not weakness
- She builds while others are still talking
- Philosophy matters more than timing

## How To Use The Story Bank

### Rule 1: Story-First, Not Topic-First
When creating content, start with a story from this bank. The story generates the insight, not the other way around.

**Example prompt:**
> "Story: The Grocery Line. Topic: How to show up as present while your business runs. Go."

The AI opens with the grocery line moment, lets it create tension naturally, then weaves the topic through.

### Rule 2: One Story Per Post (Minimum)
Every post has at least one story. If you're writing multiple posts, vary which stories you use.

### Rule 3: New Stories Get Added Immediately
When Maria-Ines shares a new story in conversation, it gets added to this list with:
- Category (what emotion/message it carries)
- Used for (what content types benefit)
- The story (word-for-word)
- What it proves/establishes
- Deployment guidance

### Rule 4: Stories Aren't Copy-Paste
The story appears in the content, but it's integrated — not just dropped in. The [[story-weave-method]] wraps the story in context so it lands as part of the message, not an interruption.

---

## Story Rotation System

When creating a batch of 6+ posts, use different stories in each. This prevents:
- Repetition (boring)
- Over-relying on one narrative
- Missing opportunities for variety

**Example rotation (6 posts):**
1. Post 1: The Venezuela Story
2. Post 2: The Grocery Line
3. Post 3: The Time-Rich CEO Flop
4. Post 4: The MCP Moment
5. Post 5: The Solo Trip
6. Post 6: The Silent Buyer
