# Pricing Philosophy — Why Things Cost What They Cost

> Pricing at Envisioned isn't based on hours, deliverables, or market rates. It's based on what the founder is currently paying — in cognitive load, missed revenue, and identity erosion — to NOT have this built.

---

## The Core Principle

The price is never about what Maria-Ines delivers. It's about what the founder gets BACK:

- **Time** she's currently spending on work that should be systematized
- **Revenue** she's leaving on the table because she can't scale past herself
- **Cognitive space** consumed by operational noise instead of strategic thinking
- **Identity** — the gap between who she is and who her business forces her to be

A founder paying herself $15/hour to do tasks that could be codified is already spending more than any Envisioned offer costs.

---

## The Ladder Logic

See [[offer-architecture]] for the full ladder. Here's why each tier is priced where it is:

### Entry Tier (€27-297): Permission to Explore

**Cowork Lab (€27-97) / Genius Map (€197-297)**

Low enough that the decision is "why not?" not "can I afford this?" The purpose isn't revenue — it's self-selection. The person who buys at this tier is testing whether Envisioned actually understands her world.

**Pricing logic:** Below the "ask my accountant" threshold. Above "free" because free attracts browsers, not buyers. The small investment creates commitment without risk.

### Diagnostic Tier (€297-3,500): See the Problem Clearly

**Decision Detox Sprint (€297-497) / Workflow Extraction (€2,500-3,500) / Capacity Mapping™ (€3,500)**

This is the X-ray. The founder pays to see what she already suspects — her business is structurally founder-dependent. The diagnosis IS the value, even if she never buys another offer.

**Pricing logic:** High enough to signal "this is serious strategic work, not a PDF." Low enough relative to the implementation tier that the diagnostic feels like a natural first step. Capacity Mapping at €3,500 is positioned as an investment that applies forward — [[offer-architecture#Bridge Language|bridge language]] ensures no sunk cost.

### Implementation Tier (€3,500-5,500): Build the Infrastructure

**The Atelier: Foundation (€3,500) / The Atelier: Build (€5,500)**

Physical. In Paris. Bespoke. The pricing reflects three things: (1) Maria-Ines's physical time and presence, (2) the exclusivity of in-person work, (3) the proof embedded — the founder's business runs while she's in Paris, which IS the demonstration.

**Pricing logic:** Aligned with high-end consulting day rates. The Paris location isn't a cost center — it's a positioning choice. "Your business runs while you're having lunch in Paris" is the most powerful proof of concept possible.

### Transformation Tier (€7,500-10,000): Full Founder Extraction

**Time Rich CEO (€7,500-10,000)**

90 days. Everything that makes the founder the bottleneck gets structurally eliminated. This is the highest-investment offer because it delivers the highest-identity outcome: from "founder who runs everything" to "Intentional CEO."

**Pricing logic:** Compared to the cost of hiring a COO ($100K+/year), or the revenue lost by remaining the bottleneck ($50K-200K/year in constrained growth), €7,500-10,000 is a fraction. The frame isn't "consulting fees" — it's "infrastructure investment with permanent ROI."

### Proof Tier (€7,497): Experience the Transformation

**Intentional CEO Sprint + Codified in the City**

Not an upsell of Time Rich CEO — a different entry to the same transformation. The pricing mirrors the transformation tier because the outcome is equivalent, but the delivery includes the experiential proof of [[codify-in-the-city|CiTC]].

## What Never Happens

- **Hourly billing.** Never. Pricing by the hour penalizes efficiency and rewards slow work.
- **Discounting.** The prices reflect the value. If someone can't afford it, they're not the ICP — they're at a different stage.
- **Comparison pricing.** Envisioned doesn't compete on price because no one else offers this integration. See [[competitive-positioning]].
- **Scope creep pricing.** Each offer has defined deliverables. More work = next rung, not a bigger invoice.

---

## The Scarcity Model

Scarcity at Envisioned is REAL, not manufactured:

- **Capacity Mapping:** Max 2 clients/month (Maria-Ines's direct involvement required)
- **The Atelier:** Limited by Paris schedule and physical presence
- **CiTC:** Limited by city timing, logistics, and cohort size
- **Time Rich CEO:** Limited by the 90-day cycle and concurrent client capacity

When Maria-Ines says "you won't see these prices again," she means it. Price increases follow demand, not marketing calendars.

---

## How to Talk About Price

In content and DMs, price is framed through contrast:

> "You're currently paying yourself $15/hour to manually do what your codified systems could handle. That's not a savings — it's the most expensive decision in your business."

Never justify the price. Name what the founder is already paying to NOT have this. The reframe: the question isn't "can I afford this?" — it's "can I afford to keep doing what I'm doing?"
