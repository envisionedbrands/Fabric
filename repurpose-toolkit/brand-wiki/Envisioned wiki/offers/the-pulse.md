# The Pulse — AI for Founders Who Lead Differently

> The entry-level relationship layer of the Envisioned ecosystem. The space that keeps a founder sharp, current, and never behind with AI — without her having to chase tools, newsletters, or hype.

**€97/month | 3-month minimum | Bi-weekly live calls | Replay library | Membership**

---

## The Promise

> "The space that keeps you sharp, current, and never behind with AI."

Not a course. Not a cohort. Not a content library. A living membership where the AI conversation stays current, applied, and grounded in founder reality.

---

## What It Actually Is

Bi-weekly live calls with Maria-Ines and a small group of founders. Each call has:

- **A topic** — the architectural or AI shift worth her attention that week
- **Case studies** — what she's seeing in client work, anonymized where needed
- **Direct troubleshooting** — members bring live stuck points, the group + Maria-Ines unlock them
- **Claude updates** — what shipped, what matters, what to migrate, what to ignore

Between calls, members have access to the **replay library** (every call recorded and searchable). Non-members can purchase **pay-per-call access** to a specific session if the topic lands for them — a low-friction door for founders still deciding.

The format is deliberately small. The conversation can't be intimate if the room is a webinar. So it isn't one.

## Who The Pulse Is For

The ideal member is a **curious founder at the awareness or consideration stage** of the [[buyer-journey]] — not yet ready for the €3,500 decision of [[codify-in-the-city]], but serious enough to pay €97/month to stay in Maria-Ines's orbit.

**She is:**

- $100K–$500K revenue (earlier than the typical CITC buyer)
- Curious, not desperate — she's watching Maria-Ines work, not in crisis
- Tired of "10 AI tools you need" newsletters — they don't clarify anything
- Wants to observe before investing deeply
- Values **staying current** over **transformation** (for now)
- Already has capacity to implement — just needs the right question, asked at the right moment, by someone she trusts

Some members are post-CITC founders who stay in The Pulse as their sustaining relationship layer. Both entry points are valid.

**Not for:**

- Beginners wanting intro-to-AI (the floor is too high)
- Founders looking for a Slack community with casual chat
- People who want passive content consumption
- Founders already ready for the €3,500 intervention — go straight to CITC

See [[ideal-client-profile]] for the Pulse-member-vs-CITC-buyer differentiation.

## The Pulse vs. Codify in the City

The Pulse and [[codify-in-the-city]] serve different purposes and shouldn't be confused.

| | **The Pulse** | **Codify in the City** |
|---|---|---|
| **Shape** | Ongoing relationship layer | One-time deep intervention |
| **Rhythm** | Bi-weekly live calls, sustained | Single VIP day |
| **Price** | €97/month (3-month minimum) | €3,500 one-time |
| **Promise** | Stay sharp, current, never behind | Codify founder intelligence into AI-ready architecture |
| **Outcome** | Implementation support, applied thinking | Installed infrastructure + 12-month roadmap |
| **When it fits** | Awareness / Consideration stage — she wants to observe | Decision stage — she's ready for foundational codification |

**The Pulse sustains. CITC transforms.** Most founders do both, sequentially: Pulse as the entry door, CITC as the deep intervention, Pulse again afterward to protect what was installed.

## Why It Matters (The Positioning)

AI changes faster than any individual founder can track. The Pulse exists because:

1. **Staying current costs founder attention.** Most founders spend 2–5 hours a week trying to keep up with AI news that doesn't apply to them. The Pulse compresses that to 1–2 hours bi-weekly of *applied* attention.
2. **Implementation stalls without live accountability.** Founders learn, then don't apply. Live calls create the forcing function.
3. **The best questions come from other founders.** Maria-Ines is in the room, but so are peers facing the same architectural decisions. The lateral learning is real.
4. **The relationship layer protects the hero offer.** CITC is a €3,500 decision. The Pulse is the €97 door. Pulse-curious founders are the natural CITC pipeline.

See [[offer-architecture]] for how The Pulse sits as the entry tier above CITC.

## Positioning vs. the Market

Most AI memberships are content libraries with a Slack channel attached. The Pulse isn't. The Pulse is the **live conversation** where a specific strategist (Maria-Ines) is in the room with a small group of founders applying AI to real businesses.

The value is Maria-Ines's attention, live, in rotation with peer conversation. Not recorded content.

## How It Connects to the Ecosystem

- **From The Pulse → CITC:** Natural path. A founder realizes she needs the foundational codification and books the VIP day.
