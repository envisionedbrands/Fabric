# Offer Architecture

The Envisioned ecosystem has been deliberately simplified. Two active heroes drive the business: **[[the-pulse|The Pulse]]** (entry tier, relationship layer) and **[[codify-in-the-city|Codified in the City]]** (hero tier, transformation). Everything else is either an active secondary (sold when the fit is right) or deprioritized (kept in the wiki, not actively marketed).

---

## The Active Ladder

```
ENTRY (relationship layer — sustained presence in her thinking)
    The Pulse — €97/month (3-month minimum)
        ↓
HERO (single deep transformation)
    Codified in the City — €3,500 (in-person VIP day)
    Codified Live — €3,500 (virtual Zoom w/ remote screen control)
        ↓
BUNDLE (full arc)
    Intentional CEO Sprint + CITC — €7,497
        ↓
DEEP (when relevant, price on application)
    Power Portraits
    Map My AI
    Envisioned Brand Experience
```

## Hero Tier (Actively Sold, Marketed, Defended)

### [[the-pulse|The Pulse]] — €97/month (3-month minimum)
**Shape:** Membership. Bi-weekly live calls, replay library, pay-per-call option for non-members.
**Promise:** The space that keeps you sharp, current, and never behind with AI.
**Who it's for:** Curious founders at awareness/consideration stage of the [[buyer-journey]]. $100K–$500K revenue. Wants to observe before investing deeply.
**Role in the ecosystem:** Entry door. Sustains relationship. Feeds natural pipeline to CITC.

### [[codify-in-the-city|Codify in the City / Codified Live]] — €3,500
**Shape:** Single VIP day (in-person in a European city OR virtual Zoom with remote screen control). Same methodology both formats.
**Promise:** Codify founder intelligence into AI-ready architecture. Installed infrastructure + 12-month roadmap in one day.
**Who it's for:** Decision-stage founder ready for foundational codification.
**Role in the ecosystem:** The one intervention that doesn't need to repeat. Deep. Transformative.

## Ladder Logic — Why This Structure

1. **Two heroes are enough.** A founder should not have to choose between nine ladder rungs. She should see two: the ongoing relationship (The Pulse) and the deep intervention (CITC). Every other offer is either a derivative of these two or deprioritized.

2. **The €97 door protects the €3,500 decision.** Most founders aren't ready for CITC on first contact. The Pulse gives them a low-friction way to stay in Maria-Ines's orbit while they decide. Without The Pulse, CITC-curious founders disappear into the content feed with no way to commit.

3. **The €3,500 transformation doesn't need to repeat.** CITC is designed as a single, complete intervention. Founders who want sustained relationship after CITC return to The Pulse. The architecture is intentionally non-ladder-greedy: no offer is designed to funnel into the next one.

4. **Bridge integrity.** Work is never wasted. A Pulse member who books CITC still has everything she's absorbed. A CITC graduate who joins The Pulse has deeper context than a new member. Everything compounds.

---

## Design Principles

1. **Two heroes, no ambiguity.** The Pulse and CITC are the headline. Everything else is secondary or archival.
2. **Ascending commitment, not ascending complexity.** The Pulse (€97/mo) → CITC (€3,500) is a simple two-step ladder, not a nine-rung funnel.
3. **Proof embedded.** The Pulse is where the CITC methodology is visible live. Members see the work being done before they buy the deep version.
4. **No sunk cost.** If a founder starts in The Pulse and upgrades to CITC, the months she's invested count as preparation, not waste.
