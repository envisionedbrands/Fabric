## The 90-Day Arc

### Phase 1: Excavation (Weeks 1-4)
**Extract everything that lives in founder's head.**

What gets excavated:
- Decision frameworks (how founder decides what matters)
- Client interaction patterns (what founder does in discovery/delivery)
- Content process (how thinking becomes published work)
- Offer evolution (how founder decides what to create)
- Rhythm and rituals (when founder does what, and why)
- Voice and positioning (how founder communicates)
- Non-negotiables (what cannot be delegated, compromised, or templated)

Output: Complete intelligence audit. Everything that makes founder irreplicable is now documented.

This phase is heavy on founder. It requires founder to think about thinking. To articulate what's usually invisible.

### Phase 2: Architecture (Weeks 5-8)
**Design infrastructure to hold everything extracted.**

What gets architected:
- **CEO Command Center** — One place where everything lives (decisions, client info, systems, roadmap)
- **Decision Infrastructure** — How decisions get made when founder isn't in the room
- **Client Experience Architecture** — What client experiences, how it's maintained without founder
- **Team Protocols** — If founder has team, how team operates without founder as filter
- **AI Integration** — Where AI amplifies vs. where it replaces, how it stays trained
- **Approval Workflows** — Where founder stays in loop, where delegation is complete

This phase is collaborative. Architect + founder design together. Founder reviews, approves, evolves.

### Phase 3: Installation (Weeks 9-12)
**Build, test, deploy, live.**

What gets installed:
- All systems go live (systems from Phase 2 architecture become operational)
- Team onboarded (if applicable, team learns new workflows)
- Founder steps back progressively (Week 9: 80% involved. Week 10: 60%. Week 11: 40%. Week 12: Director only)
- Real business runs through new systems (not test data, actual clients/work)
- Adjustments made in real time (systems refined based on live use)

By Week 12: Business runs without founder-dependency. Founder is director, not operator.

### Phase 4: Integration (Weeks 13-ongoing)
**Proof, evolution, sustainability.**

What's integrated:
- **Codify in the City** (optional) — Day 60 proof experience (some programs include this)
- **30-day observation** — Architect watches systems, identifies evolution points
- **Quarterly reviews** — Infrastructure reviewed, evolved, scaled
- **Team evolution** — If team involved, continued development happens here
- **Ongoing support** — 6 months post-program access to architect for questions/evolution

4. **AI Memory (CLAUDE.md)** — Custom AI trained on founder's methodology, voice, decisions
5. **Operational Playbooks** — How everything works, who does what, when, why
6. **Team Protocols** (if applicable) — How team operates, escalates, makes decisions
7. **12-Month Roadmap** — Strategic direction for next year, phases, milestones

Everything documented. Everything live. Everything tested with real business.

## Common Questions

**"Can I do this while running my business?"**
Yes. Excavation phase is heavy (leader needs to be present). Phases 2-4 integrate into business.

**"What if I don't have a team?"**
Absolutely fine. Systems work for solo founders. Infrastructure holds without team.

**"What if my business is very dependent on me?"**
That's exactly why you're here. After 90 days, it won't be.

**"Do I need to use specific tools?"**
No. We work with your existing tech stack. Integrate with what's already there.

**"What happens after 90 days?"**
Business runs. You maintain it. Quarterly evolution calls keep it optimized.
