# Competitive Positioning — The White Space

## The Market Exists in 4 Types (Envisioned IS Type 4)

### Type 1: The Technician
**What they do:** Tool lists, tutorials, prompts, ChatGPT templates
- **Identity:** "Here's what you can do with this AI"
- **Founder dependency:** UNCHANGED — founder still runs everything
- **Price:** Free to $99/month
- **Examples:** Generic AI "prompt engineers," free tutorials

**Why this fails:** Gives you a hammer, not a house.

### Type 2: The Brand Strategist
**What they do:** Positioning, voice, brand identity, messaging frameworks
- **Identity:** "Here's who you are"
- **Founder dependency:** UNCHANGED — business still in founder's head
- **Price:** $5k-$25k projects
- **Examples:** Traditional brand agencies, positioning consultants

**Why this fails:** Beautiful strategy, invisible execution.

### Type 3: The Hybrid (Surface Level)
**What they do:** "AI-powered brand strategy" — positioning + AI tools
- **Identity:** "Let's use AI to amplify your brand"
- **Founder dependency:** UNCHANGED (slightly reduced) — surface amplification, no infrastructure
- **Price:** $50k-$150k
- **Examples:** High-end "AI consultants," brand agencies adding AI services
- **Weakness:** Amplification without infrastructure is just noise at higher velocity

**Why this fails:** Amplifies noise instead of essence.

### Type 4: The Freedom Architect ← **THIS IS ENVISIONED**
**What they do:** All three pillars integrated — extraction + codification + deployment
- **Identity:** "Here's your infrastructure that makes the founder optional"
- **Founder dependency:** RADICALLY REDUCED — business runs because it's architected
- **Price:** $50k-$250k (premium for premium transformation)
- **Examples:** ONLY Envisioned positioned this way currently

**Why this dominates:** Nobody else is here. White space = zero direct competitors.
