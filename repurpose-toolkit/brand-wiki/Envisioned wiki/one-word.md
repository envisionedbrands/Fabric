# One Word

**Problem:** Generic
**Solution:** Codified

**The sentence:** Envisioned exists because uncodified genius comes out generic; everything we make moves it toward codified.

**The lens:** every piece of content names Generic or advances Codified. Content that does neither is off-brand by definition.

**USAGE RULE (2026-07-03, per Maria-Ines — non-negotiable):** Generic NEVER describes the woman. She is not generic; nothing about her is. Generic describes the OUTPUT — what her brilliance becomes when it passes uncodified through tools, teams, and the machine layer. The diagnosis is always structural, never personal: "You're not generic. Your output is — because your genius isn't codified yet." This is the remove-the-blame rule applied to the lens: name the architecture, never the character. Any draft that lets "generic" touch her identity gets rewritten.

**Generic means:** what uncodified brilliance turns into on its way out. The offer maps that sound like everyone else's offer maps. The sales page with the rhythm of a machine that has never met her methodology. Her clients filling the gap between calls with ChatGPT output she can spot instantly — because the tool doesn't know her work exists. The program that teaches brilliantly and delivers like it's 2021. What her essence becomes in the machine layer when it isn't codified: to LLMs and agents she doesn't exist, so they serve up everyone-else.

**Codified means:** her methodology extracted and structured so it is legible to humans, teams, LLMs, and agents. Her standards in every room she isn't in. Her clients' output unmistakably hers. Her essence amplified — never replaced — by the machine layer. The badge she already wears: *Codified* (the Substack), Codified in the City, Codified Live, DM "CODIFY."

**Runners-up considered:**

- Bottleneck → Codified — passed all five tests but named the ops symptom, not the identity-level buy; the bullseye ICP doesn't complain about ops chaos.
- Behind → Codified — the fear word (Holly verbatim, her own post title); lost because it names the anxiety, not the evidence she can see. Keep "behind" as a hook word in content.
- Invisible → Amplified — the machine-layer problem and her own outcome word; lost because amplified is what codified earns, not the lens itself.
- Dependency → Architecture — the gold line embodies it, but "dependency" head-tilts and "architecture" is a contested consultant word.
- Invisible → Codified — tight mirror, folded into what Generic covers with more felt evidence.
- Performance → Presence — the philosophy pair; presence stays the point word of the brand, not the sorting lens.
- Bottleneck → Infrastructure — both words verbatim in the room, but mechanism-flavored on both sides.
- Effort → Design — the thesis Binary; not her 11pm words, and "design" is rented.
- Trapped → Freedom — strongest felt problem word, but "freedom" is owned by the whole industry (incl. Bossbabe's Freedom Engine™).
- Noise → Essence — the oldest reframe; "noise" too soft on the felt test.
- Required → Held — poetic and hers ("a business that holds you"), but "held" risks coach-speak register.
