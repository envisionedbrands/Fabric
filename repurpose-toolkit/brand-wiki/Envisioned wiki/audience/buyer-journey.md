# Buyer Journey — From Silent Lurker to Intentional CEO

> The complete path from first contact to transformation. Envisioned's buyer rarely engages publicly. She watches, decides internally, then moves fast. The journey is designed for this behavior.

---

## The Silent Buyer Pattern

Maria-Ines's highest-converting buyers share a behavior: they never liked a post. Never commented. Never engaged publicly. Then one day, a DM: "I want Milan." See [[story-bank#The Silent Buyer]].

This isn't a failure of engagement strategy. It's the proof that the positioning works. The content acts as a filter — it speaks directly to someone experiencing [[ideal-client-profile#The Core Problem The Loop|The Loop]], and she self-selects without needing to be nurtured.

**Implication for content:** Public engagement metrics undercount actual pipeline. DM volume and quality are the real KPIs.

---

## Stage 1: Awareness — She Feels the Gap

**Trigger events:**
- Revenue grew but exhaustion grew faster
- Hired a VA/team member and realized she can't explain her process
- Tried an AI tool, got underwhelming results, blamed herself
- Another "system" implementation that changed nothing
- Saw someone else (with worse work) scaling past her

**What she believes at this stage:**
"There's something structural wrong with my business — not just tactical."

**How Envisioned appears:**
Content that names The Loop without offering a quick fix. Philosophy posts. The [[the-codification-thesis|Codification Thesis]] reframe. Venezuelan lens stories. She reads these and thinks "this person actually understands what I'm dealing with."

**Content doors that work here:** Ethics/Identity, Philosophy, Myth-Busting. See [[content-strategy#The 6 Doors]].

**First offer available at this stage:** [[the-pulse|The Pulse]] (€97/mo, 3-month minimum). She isn't ready for CITC, but The Pulse gives her a low-friction way to stay in Maria-Ines's orbit while the thinking takes root. Previously this stage had "no offer yet" — The Pulse now closes that gap.

---

## Stage 2: Consideration — Can Anyone Actually Do This?

**What she's researching:**
- Is it possible to extract what's in my head?
- Who does this? (Usually nobody at this level)
- What does the process actually look like?
- What would success even feel like?

**The fear:** "This is too big to fix" or "This is too specific to my business."

**What she needs:** Proof. Not testimonials — architectural proof. Case studies showing methodology extraction. The [[codify-in-the-city|Codified in the City]] concept. Evidence that the before/after is real and structural.

**How Envisioned shows up:**
Case Study content door. Positioning door. Offer-specific posts that show what the work looks like from inside. The grocery-line story. The MCP moment.

**The threshold question she's asking herself:** "Is this person going to understand MY specific situation, or is this another template?"

**Offer available at this stage:** [[the-pulse|The Pulse]] (€97/mo) — the live-call room where she can observe the work in action before deciding on the €3,500 intervention. Pulse membership is how many consideration-stage founders convert to CITC buyers over 3–6 months.

---

## Stage 3: Decision — The DM

**How it happens:**
She doesn't fill out a form. She doesn't book a "discovery call" from a link in bio. She DMs. Often with a single word or sentence:
- "I want Milan"
- "CODIFY"
- "I've been watching your content for months. I'm ready."

**What shifted her:**
- Understanding this isn't adding another person or tool — it's intelligence transfer
- Seeing that the infrastructure gets built FOR her, not with her as the doer
- A specific piece of content that named her exact situation
- Permission from the content to want both legacy AND living

**The conversation in DMs:**
Not sales. Diagnosis. Maria-Ines asks what their business looks like, where they're stuck, what they've tried. She's qualifying while they feel heard. The diagnosis IS the pitch — by the time she names the offer, the founder already sees herself in it.

**Critical:** No hard sell. No urgency tactics. No "only 3 spots left." The scarcity is real (max 2 Capacity Mapping clients/month, Atelier is physically limited by Paris schedule). The filter is the DM keyword itself — only serious people use it.

---

## Stage 4: Implementation — Cognitive Relief

**The first win:**
Something that was always manual is now running. An AI system that responds the way the founder would. A workflow that doesn't need her. The first proof that her intelligence can exist outside her body.

**The emotional sequence:**
1. Relief: "Something is actually changing"
2. Vulnerability: "This person can see how messy my systems are"
3. Recognition: "Oh — this is how I actually think"
4. Excitement: "This is what my business could feel like"

**The identity moment:** She sees her own methodology extracted and structured for the first time. This isn't just operational — it's existential. "This is how I think" made visible.

---

## Stage 5: Transformation — Becomes Intentional CEO

**What shifts:**
- Business runs on [[the-codification-thesis|codified intelligence]], not founder execution
- Can take actual breaks (not "time off while checking phone")
- Team operates from documented standards instead of proximity
- Pricing reflects value, not effort
- Founder has cognitive space for vision, not operations

**The identity shift:**
From "founder who runs a business" → "founder whose business runs intelligently."

She becomes an [[legacy-living-spin|Intentional CEO]]. And then she tells someone. In a DM.
