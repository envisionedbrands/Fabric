# Indications of Life Theme — Tender Moments, Motherhood, Home

> Visual theme for the life AROUND the work. Signals motherhood, partnership, rural Dutch home, presence. The proof of the "time-rich CEO" promise.

---

## Why This Theme Exists

Maria-Ines's whole philosophy ([[legacy-living-spin]], Time-Rich CEO, Intentional CEO) is about building a business that gives her her LIFE back. The audience needs to **see** that life to believe the promise. Without visual evidence, the philosophy reads as theory.

This theme provides visual evidence:

- Part-time solo mom
- Partner (Dave)
- Rural Dutch home (Dreumel area)
- Seasonal rhythms (spring lambs, baby animals, willow trees, canal edges)
- Tender, quiet, non-staged

---

## ETHICAL CONSTRAINT — CRITICAL

**Never generate her daughter's likeness. Never show her daughter's face.**

Maria-Ines does NOT show her daughter's face on public content. The child's privacy is protected. AI must respect this absolutely.

### What IS Allowed

- Back of a small child walking ahead (generic silhouette, no identifying features)
- A small hand held by Maria-Ines's larger hand (hand only, no face)
- A small shoe, small jacket, drawing, toy (object only)
- An empty child-sized chair, half-eaten toast, kids' shoes by the door
- A child-height shadow cast on a floor
- A small figure in the far distance (Dutch landscape wide shot with hint of a child running ahead)

### What Is NOT Allowed

- Her daughter's face, ever
- Close-ups where identifying features would be visible
- Detailed likeness generation of the child
- Full-body front-facing child shots where expression/features are recognizable

---

## Partner (Dave) — Similar But Softer

She hasn't explicitly ruled out Dave's face, but the default is **hinted presence, not featured.** Back of head, hand on table, shadow, coffee cup beside hers, jacket on the chair. If she explicitly asks for him in an image, treat as its own case.

---

## Setting Vocabulary (Dutch Rural, Spring Default)

Dreumel / middle of the Netherlands / rural spring. Visual hooks:

- Lambs in fields (spring baby animals — recurring moment she references)
- Willow trees
- Canal edges, canal-side cobblestone
- Dutch farmhouse vernacular (thatched roof, brick barn, weathered wood)
- Flat polder landscape with big sky
- Spring blossoms (cherry, apple, blackthorn)
- Morning fog over fields
- Bicycle leaning against a wall (subtle, NOT the cliché Amsterdam bike shot)
- Stone garden walls, old stone paths
- Windmill on horizon (ONLY if it reads local, not touristy Keukenhof)

---

## Tone / Mood

- Quiet, not loud
- Witnessed, not staged
- Local, not exotic
- Seasonal — spring right now; update for summer/autumn/winter throughout the year
- Dolce far niente adjacent but distinct: dolce far niente is about REST; this theme is about LIFE (movement, child's pace, seasonal rhythm)

---

## Use Cases

- Instagram Stories (9:16 fragments of her actual life)
- Newsletter soft moments
- Essays about motherhood, rest, the WHY of her business
- Paid content (these hit differently than generic luxury imagery — they're SPECIFIC)
- Top-of-funnel brand building (the "I want that life" pull)

---

## Image Types to Generate

1. **Back-of-child walking ahead** — small child silhouette walking down a tree-lined lane, spring, she's slightly behind (optional — can be no one, just the child-hinted path)
2. **Small hand in her hand** — close-up of her hand holding a small hand, walking together, no faces
3. **Dutch spring landscape** — lambs in a green field, willow tree, polder, wide sky
4. **Two coffee cups at a kitchen table** — evidence of two (her and Dave), no people
5. **Child's jacket on a hook** — small jacket, boots by the door, afternoon light through a window
6. **Quiet morning reading** — her on a couch with a book, light coming through a window, small shoes on the floor nearby
7. **Shadow of a child running** — cast on a stone path, she's in frame behind (optional)
8. **Spring blossoms framing a stone wall** — no people, just the evidence of the place
9. **Bicycle leaning against a brick wall** — morning, spring, stillness
10. **Canal at blue hour** — calm water, a few lights, the stillness of a small Dutch town in the evening

## Integration With the Library

This theme sits alongside Dolce Far Niente but is distinct:

- **Dolce Far Niente** = REST (evening, wine, book, nothing to do)
- **Indications of Life** = LIFE (morning, family, seasonal, present)

Build as theme folder: `01-BRAND/visual-themes/indications-of-life/`

---

## How to Apply

- Apply same CD rules as other themes (no scarf, hand spec, Italian Afternoon grade — see [[visual-generation-rules]])
- No face reference needed since Maria-Ines is often not the subject or only hinted
- When she IS in frame, use the identity pack from [[visual-assets-locations]]
- When her daughter would be in frame, enforce the ethical constraint (hinted / back-view / fragment only)
- When Dave would be in frame, default to hinted presence
