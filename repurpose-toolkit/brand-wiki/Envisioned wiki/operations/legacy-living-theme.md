# Legacy Living Theme — The Visual Proof of the Brand Promise

> The theme that sells the offer. The life that's possible when AI (Liberty) does the work. Not working scenes — **living** scenes.

For the **philosophy** behind Legacy Living, see [[legacy-living-spin]]. This article covers the **visual** vocabulary only.

---

## The Thesis (Maria-Ines's Exact Words, 2026-04-19)

> "Building a legacy and still have time for lunch — that to me is the epitome of dolce far niente."

This is the brand's creative throughline made visual. The offers ([[codify-in-the-city|CITC]], Intentional CEO Sprint) sell the WORK. This theme sells the RESULT — what life looks like when Liberty (her AI assistant) handles the work she does not want to do.

**This is the theme that converts.**

---

## What This Theme IS

- The LIFE the audience aspires to
- "Lived-in stock" aesthetic — feels like a real moment caught
- Aspirational without being unattainable
- Mediterranean / European / warm
- Often featuring her, sometimes featuring other women per [[representation-rules]]
- The viewer should feel "I want THAT"

## What This Theme IS NOT

- Working at a desk (that's Workspace theme)
- Quiet solitary rest with no markers (that's Dolce Far Niente)
- Family / motherhood / Dutch home (that's [[indications-of-life-theme]])
- Travel / movement (that's Cinematic Travel)

**The distinction:** Legacy Living is **active leisure** — eating, reading, swimming, walking, gathering. Not stillness. Not work. Living.

---

## Core Motifs (Validated List)

### Hero Motifs (Highest Priority)

- **Cafés** — sitting at a small table, espresso, croissant, journal closed
- **Leisurely lunches** — long table, wine, friends/partner, food, outdoor or warm interior
- **Pool / water** — floating, reading by the pool, infinity edge with view
- **Bath / robe / morning slow** — silk robe (NOT blazer), bath with candle, slow coffee
- **Walking / hiking** — through landscape, no phone visible
- **Reading** — hammock, garden, balcony, sunlit chair

### Supporting Motifs

- Picking flowers / garden tending
- Long breakfast on a balcony
- Bare feet on warm stone
- Wine at sunset
- Slow cooking (visible enjoyment, not work)
- Conversation across a table

---

## Excluded

- **NO napping** (her preference — see [[personal-preferences]])
- **NO blazers** (her preference)
- **NO scarves** (her preference)
- **NO laptop / phone in frame** (defeats the thesis)
- **NO emerald green** (brand rule — though sage/fern green in nature/decor is fine)

---

## Connection to Her Offers

- **[[codify-in-the-city|CITC]] (€3,500 VIP day)** — the post-VIP "you can leave the laptop closed" promise
- **Intentional CEO Sprint** — the "Time-Rich CEO" outcome
- **[[the-pulse|The Pulse]]** — the lifestyle that members are observing Maria-Ines build
- **Envisioned Brand Experience** — the lifestyle of having done the brand work
- **Future Substack paid tier** — the "what your life looks like" aspirational pull

When CITC is being launched, this theme provides the visual content. When nurture sequences run, this theme is the inevitability she's selling.

---

## Demographic Distribution

Same as [[representation-rules]]:
- Maria-Ines herself (when scene allows)
- Black, Latina, Asian, mixed-heritage women
- ~1% white women maximum
- Range of ages and presentations within "women" bucket

---

## Lighting / Color Signature

- Mediterranean afternoon light (warm, soft)
- OR European morning (slightly cooler, fresh)
- Italian Afternoon grade applied
- Warm neutrals, terracotta, cream, honey, soft sage in nature/decor
- Avoid: bright midday sun, harsh contrast, overly polished commercial finish

See [[brand-strategy]] for the canonical color palette.
