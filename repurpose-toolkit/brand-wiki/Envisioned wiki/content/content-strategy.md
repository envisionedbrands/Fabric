# Content Strategy — The System

The content strategy for Envisioned Brands. Not about "posting more." About building coherent philosophy into every piece.

## The ONE Message

Every post points here. This is the North Star thesis.

> **AI isn't a productivity hack — it's a mirror that forces you to decide who you are when effort no longer proves worth.**

All content, regardless of format or platform, is a different door into this single truth. Not variations on the theme. Not related topics. Variations on THIS thesis.

## The Story-Weave Method

The structure every post uses. Borrowed from Suzi Gray's mechanics, executed in María-Inés's voice.

### Step 1: MOMENT
Open with something that happened. A scene, a conversation, a memory, an observation.

Put the reader INSIDE it before they know what the post is about.

**Examples:**
- "I connected a tool to my business this morning and my eyes welled up."
- "I grew up watching systems fail. Not metaphorically. I'm Venezuelan."
- "Yesterday I built a launch package from the grocery line while waiting for M."

**Rule:** No announcing. No "Listen" or "Here's what happened." Just the moment.

### Step 2: TENSION
The story naturally surfaces a gap or contradiction. A question. A problem.

Don't announce it ("Here's the thing..."). Let the story create it.

**Example:**
Story: "I connected a tool to my business and it understood my methodology."
Tension (implicit): "How is that possible? What made it understand?"

### Step 3: REFRAME
Connect the moment to the deeper truth. Identity. Legacy. AI philosophy. Design principles.

This lands BECAUSE of the story, not despite it. The reader has context. They're inside the moment.

**Example:**
"The tool didn't do this. I did. The reason it was powerful is because I'd already extracted my methodology, mapped my offers, documented my decision patterns. The AI had something to connect TO."

### Step 4: CONVICTION
One clear, undeniable statement. The punchline the whole post built toward.

No hedging. No "you might" or "consider." This is the truth.

**Example:**
"Codified intelligence scales. Founder-dependency doesn't."

### Step 5: FILTER
Permission-based CTA that self-selects serious people.

"DM WORD" or nothing at all.

**Examples:**
- "DM CODIFY if you're ready for the last AI partner you'll need"
- "Comment DESIGN if you're tired of rebuilding from scratch every time"
- (No CTA — post stands alone)

## The 6 Doors Rotation System

Never make a post "about" an angle. Each angle is a different emotional door into the ONE message.

Borrowed from Agus di Carlo's system.

### Door 1: ETHICS/IDENTITY
Energy: "I refuse to build things that expire"

Lead with conviction about what matters.

**Example entries:**
- Venezuela story (watching systems fail)
- Markdown philosophy (what lasts)
- Refusing to build on platforms that lock you in

**Thesis connection:** "AI forces identity reckoning. If you build thoughtfully, you build legacy."

### Door 2: MYTH-BUSTING
Energy: "What you've been told is backwards"

Name the assumption. Flip it.

**Example entries:**
- "AI doesn't save time. It makes you face yourself."
- "You don't have a visibility problem. You have a positioning problem."
- VA promoted story (scaling doesn't mean more people)

**Thesis connection:** "The real problem isn't tools. It's who you become when tools make you unnecessary."

### Door 3: CASE STUDY/PROOF
Energy: "Here's what happened"

Show evidence of the thesis working.

**Example entries:**
- Milan DM (silent buyer recognizing genius)
- Client transformations (codified = scalable)
- Agent architecture video (load distribution pattern)

**Thesis connection:** "When you codify, three things happen..."

### Door 4: PHILOSOPHY
Energy: "The deeper question no one's asking"

Sit in the paradox. Name the invisible problem.

**Example entries:**
- The bleed (effort proving worth)
- Founder-dependency (invisible infrastructure cost)
- Legacy living (you can scale AND have time)

**Thesis connection:** "This is actually about how you know your worth."

### Door 5: POSITIONING
Energy: "This is why my work is different"

Name the structural difference in approach.

**Example entries:**
- MCP moment (business understanding in plain English)
- Codified maps vs. templates
- Strategic relationships vs. tool stacking

**Thesis connection:** "I don't sell AI. I sell codified intelligence."

### Door 6: MOVEMENT/VISION
Energy: "This is bigger than my business"

Position the philosophy as cultural/systemic.

**Example entries:**
- Pentagon + ethics livestream (people leaning in, not away)
- Time-Rich CEO flop → rebuild (market wasn't ready, now it is)
- Intentional CEO movement (collective shift from always-on)

**Thesis connection:** "We're building the way forward."

### The Rotation Rule
In any batch of 6 posts, use all 6 doors. Never repeat the same door twice in a row. This creates content that feels varied while staying on thesis.

**Weekly rotation example:**
- Monday: Myth-Busting
- Wednesday: Philosophy
- Friday: Case Study

Next week:
- Monday: Ethics/Identity
- Wednesday: Positioning
- Friday: Movement/Vision

## Psychological Anchoring

Name the DESIGN PRINCIPLE or SYSTEMS CONCEPT underneath the story. Not psychology jargon. AI architecture language.

**Examples of anchoring:**
- "Codified intelligence layer" (what's actually happening)
- "Operational infrastructure" (why it matters)
- "Perishable genius" (what breaks)
- "The interface between founder and business is now conversation" (paradigm shift)

**Rule:** Give the insight an intellectual anchor so it's not just a feeling. It's a framework.

## What to Borrow from Each Influence

### From Suzi Gray
**TAKE the mechanic:**
- Story-first openings
- Poetic rhythm
- Quiet conviction
- The way she uses silence as punctuation

**NEVER take the voice:**
- Don't write in her gentle, invitational tone
- María-Inés is more confrontational
- Don't sound like you're asking permission
- Don't soften. Affirm.

### From Agus di Carlo
**TAKE the mechanic:**
- 6-door rotation system
- Offer-heavy distribution
- Repetition rule (short. medium. short. long.)
- Conviction > tactics
- Permission-based CTAs

**NEVER take the voice:**
- Don't write in Spanish sales energy
- Don't teach sales psychology
- Don't lead with "imagine..."
- Don't perform excitement
- Don't use "let me share something..."

### From Niché Arlow
**TAKE the mechanic:**
- Name the framework
- One-sentence paragraphs for power
- Vulnerability + authority in same breath
- Keyword CTAs

**NEVER take the voice:**
- Don't use psychology jargon
- Don't use circus energy
- Don't lead with neuroscience
- Don't make it personal to you (make it personal to principle)
- Don't oversell the accessibility
