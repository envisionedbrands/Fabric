# The 7 Content Pillars

The seven content pillars from analysis of 80 Instagram posts (Genius Report, March 2026).

These are the themes María-Inés naturally gravitates toward. Not mandates. Not "post about these 7 things." These are the philosophical throughlines that appear across her strongest content.

## Overview

| Pillar | Frequency | Key Message | Best Format |
|--------|-----------|-------------|------------|
| **AI_TRANSFORMATION** | 78.8% | "Identity trained to prove worth through effort, not design" | Reframe posts, question hooks, philosophical statements |
| **REVOLUTION** | 43.8% | "The future isn't more. It's better." | Vision posts, future positioning, call to movement |
| **FOUNDER_CEO** | 42.5% | "You've built a job, not a business" | Economic insights, diagnostic posts, framework reveals |
| **COMMAND_CENTER** | 35.0% | "Your genius should work for you, not you for it" | Product positioning, system reveals, offer statements |

## Pillar 1: AI_TRANSFORMATION
**Frequency:** 78.8% (highest appearing)

**Core thesis:**
AI doesn't change what you do. It changes who you have to be.

**Key message:**
Identity trained to prove worth through effort, not design.

**What this pillar addresses:**
- The identity reckoning AI forces
- Founder-dependency and what it costs
- Scalability through codified intelligence
- The mirror AI holds up
- Effort as proof vs. design as proof

**Best formats:**
- Reframe posts ("This isn't X, it's Y")
- Question hooks that surface the identity problem
- Philosophical statements
- Diagnostic posts
- Before/after comparisons of mindset

**Example posts in this pillar:**
- "AI isn't a productivity hack. It's a mirror."
- "Your VA knows more about your business than you do. That's the problem."
- "The moment you codify your genius..."
- "You can't outsource what isn't documented."

**CTAs that land here:**
- "DM DESIGN if..."
- "DM CODIFY if..."
- "Comment MIRROR if..."

## Key Analytics Findings

### Content Performance
**Avg caption length:** 895 characters (sweet spot: 850-900)
**Voice consistency:** 91.2% CAPS emphasis (highest of tested accounts)

### CAPS Usage
CAPS are used for:
- Philosophical emphasis (not urgency)
- Key concepts and frameworks
- Identity and conviction

**Examples:**
- IDENTITY trained to prove worth
- DESIGN not EFFORT
- COMMAND CENTER
- RESET
- AI PROBLEM
- AMPLIFIED ESSENCE

### Capitalize These Concepts
- IDENTITY
- RESET
- AI PROBLEM
- COMMAND CENTER
- DESIGN
- FOUNDER
- INFRASTRUCTURE
- LEGACY
- INTENTIONAL
- AMPLIFY / AMPLIFIED

Do NOT over-capitalize. Selective emphasis is more powerful than everything caps.

## Cross-Pillar Combinations

The strongest posts combine 2-3 pillars:

**AI_TRANSFORMATION + FOUNDER_CEO:**
"You built a job. AI shows you what a business would look like if you let it be one."

**REVOLUTION + LEGACY:**
"Time-rich CEO isn't lazy. It's legacy thinking."

**BRAND_STRATEGY + CONVICTION:**
"You don't need to be different. You need to be clear."

**COMMAND_CENTER + FOUNDER_CEO:**
"Your business doesn't need another hire. It needs codified intelligence."

## Using This Framework

**Don't:**
- Treat pillars as separate topics
- Post "about" each pillar
- Use pillars as checklist for diversity

**Do:**
- Let content naturally fall into these categories
- Combine pillars for depth
- Use this as a lens to see patterns (is content balanced across pillars?)
- Notice which pillars get strongest engagement (adapt accordingly)
