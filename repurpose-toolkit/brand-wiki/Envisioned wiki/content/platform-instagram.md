# Platform Playbook — Instagram

## Why Instagram Matters for Envisioned

Instagram is the DISCOVERY and POSITIONING platform. It's where the [[ideal-client-profile]] first encounters the brand, forms an impression, and either scrolls past or DMs "CODIFY." It's not the depth platform (that's Substack) — it's the frequency and authority platform. The silent buyer pattern lives here: they never like a post, then one day they DM asking about Milan.

## How the Algorithm Works (2026)

Instagram head Adam Mosseri confirmed three signals that matter most:

1. **Watch time** — how long people engage with your content
2. **Likes per reach** — engagement quality relative to distribution
3. **Sends per reach** — how often content gets shared via DM (carries the MOST weight for reaching new audiences)

DM shares are the #1 signal for Explore and Reels distribution. Content that makes people want to send it to a friend gets significantly more distribution than content that gets likes or comments.

### Additional Ranking Signals
- **Saves** — signal lasting value, boost extended distribution
- **Originality** — the algorithm prioritizes content that feels fresh and made for Instagram, not recycled from elsewhere
- **Keyword SEO** — keywords in captions and profiles now outperform hashtags for discovery

## Content Format Strategy

### The 40/30/30 Mix
| Format | % of Content | Purpose |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Reels | 40% | Reach — getting discovered by new audiences |
| Stories | 30% | Nurture — staying top of mind with existing audience |
| Carousels | 30% | Education & authority — deep value, saves, shares |

### Reels (Discovery Engine)
- **Sweet spot: 30-90 seconds** for educational content — highest engagement and views
- 7-15 seconds for trending/punchy content
- Up to 3 minutes now eligible for Explore recommendations
- Structure: Hook (first 3 seconds) → Proof → 2-5 steps → CTA
- Educational deep-dives and mini-vlogs can now go viral beyond your follower base
- Original content with trending audio outperforms reshared content

### Carousels (Authority Builder)
- **Average 10% engagement rate** — beats single images (7%) and Reels (6%)
- Optimal length: **6-10 slides** (peak reach around slide 13 for longer sequences)
- Hook viewers in first 3 slides, end with saveable recap or clear CTA
- Instagram now allows up to 20 slides per carousel
- The algorithm reads swipe-through rate as a quality signal — design for swipes, not just scrolls

### Stories (Relationship Layer)
- Use polls, questions, and sliders for engagement signals
- Behind-the-scenes, day-in-the-life, and real-time moments
- Link stickers for driving traffic to Substack, offers, etc.
- Story replies feed DM conversations → sales pipeline

## Caption Strategy (Major 2026 Shift)

### Keywords > Hashtags
- Instagram now functions as a search engine — captions are indexed like web content
- Both Google and Bing index Instagram captions for external search results
- Place relevant keywords directly in captions, bio, and alt text
- Use natural long-tail keyword phrases woven into the writing

### Hashtags (Reduced Role)
- Use **3-5 highly relevant hashtags** — the 30-hashtag era is over
- Hashtags now function as categorization tools, not discovery drivers
- Place in caption body or first comment — both work
- Suggested: #intentionalceo #ethicalai #aiforfounders #brandstrategy #legacyliving

### Caption Length
- **150-300 words** for carousels (long-form filters for serious audience)
- First 125 characters must hook — that's what shows before "more"
- 800-1200 characters for philosophical posts — aligns with [[content-strategy]] long-caption approach

## Posting Cadence

- **3-5 feed posts per week** (mix of Reels and carousels)
- **Daily Stories** (even if just 2-3 frames)
- **1 Reel minimum per week** for algorithmic reach
- Best times: Test your own audience, but general benchmarks point to weekday mornings (8-11 AM) and evenings (7-9 PM)

## What Works on Instagram (Maria-Inés Application)

- **Carousels for the [[6 Doors]] rotation** — each slide builds the argument, the swipe rate signals quality
- **Reels for the MOMENT** — the first 3 seconds of a Reel IS the story-first opening from [[Story-Weave Method]]
- **DM funnels over public engagement** — the brand converts through DMs, not comments. Every 3rd post should include a "DM [WORD]" CTA
- **Saves and shares > likes** — design content people want to reference later or send to a founder friend
- **Permission-based CTAs** — "Comment CODIFY" / "DM INFRASTRUCTURE" — these are psychological filters that self-select serious people

## What to Avoid

- Hashtag farming (30 hashtags, engagement pods)
- Engagement-bait questions ("What do you think?")
- Recycling content from other platforms without adapting format
- Posting single static images as primary content — lowest engagement format
- Ignoring DMs — this is where the silent buyer converts

## Envisioned-Specific Tactics

1. **Carousel = mini-Substack** — take one essay and distill it into 8 slides using the MOMENT → TENSION → REFRAME → CONVICTION structure
2. **Reels = the hook** — first 3 seconds should be the boldest claim from the post. "AI isn't a productivity hack." Cut. Then explain.
3. **Stories = daily proof of [[Legacy Living]]** — the grocery-line business, the solo trip, the quiet afternoon. This IS the product.
4. **Alt text on every post** — write keyword-rich alt text for SEO and accessibility
5. **Insight cards** — turn [[glossary]] terms into shareable single-image posts for virality

## Key Metrics to Track

- Sends per reach (DM shares — the #1 signal)
- Save rate per post
- Profile visits → DM conversion
- Reels reach vs. follower count
- Carousel swipe-through rate
