# The 6 Content Angles — Rotation System

Six primary angles to rotate for freshness while staying on thesis. These are different APPROACHES to the same core message, not different topics.

All of these land the same truth: "AI forces identity reckoning."

The rotation keeps content varied while the philosophy stays consistent.

## Angle 1: PROVOCATIVE REFRAME (70% of posts)

**Formula:** "Everyone thinks X. But really Y."

**Energy:** Sharp correction. "This is backwards from how you think about it."

**Best days:** Monday (opens thinking), Wednesday (mid-week pivot)

**Frequency:** 2-3x per week

**Structure:**
1. Name the false assumption (what people believe)
2. Name the true pattern (what's actually happening)
3. Show why the shift matters
4. State conviction

**Examples:**
- "You don't have a visibility problem. You have a positioning problem."
- "This isn't about tools. It's about who you become when tools make you unnecessary."
- "AI doesn't save time. It makes you face yourself."
- "You can't scale effort. You can scale design."
- "Authenticity ≠ Vulnerability."

**Why it lands:**
Recognition. The reader realizes they've been thinking about it wrong. Relief follows.

**CTA approach:**
"DM [WORD] if..." — Permission-based, targets serious people

**Example CTA:**
"DM POSITION if you're tired of competing on visibility instead of clarity."

---

## Angle 2: QUESTION-DRIVEN DIAGNOSIS

**Formula:** Ask a question that reveals a problem they're experiencing

**Energy:** "Wait... that's actually my situation"

**Frequency:** 1x per week

**Best days:** Tuesday or Thursday (mid-week check-in)

**Structure:**
1. Ask the diagnostic question (situation-specific)
2. Name what the answer means (diagnosis)
3. Hint at the solution (don't announce)
4. Conviction or frame-shift

**Examples:**
- "How much of your business disappears if you take a week off?"
- "What happens to your offers when you're not there to sell them?"
- "When's the last time your VA didn't need retraining on your process?"
- "If you don't have it documented, do you actually have a system?"

**Why it lands:**
Self-recognition. High DM response. High saves (people bookmark the question).

**CTA approach:**
"DM [WORD] if..." or "Comment [WORD] if the answer is 'I don't know'"

**Example CTA:**
"Comment SYSTEM if the answer is 'nowhere.'"

---

## Angle 3: FRAMEWORK REVEAL

**Formula:** Show the structure behind the idea

**Energy:** "Oh, I see how this fits together"

**Frequency:** 1-2x per month

**Best days:** Any day (frameworks are timeless)

**Structure:**
1. Name what people confuse
2. Show the actual structure (the framework)
3. Show why structure matters
4. Application or conviction

**Examples:**
- "Three circles of scaling" (effort, system, infrastructure)
- "The Effort vs. Design binary"
- "What happens when you codify"
- "The Command Center and what lives inside it"
- "The difference between visibility and positioning"

**Why it lands:**
Clarity. The reader suddenly sees the system they were feeling but not seeing.

**CTA approach:**
"DM [WORD] if you want to see this applied to your business"

**Example CTA:**
"DM FRAMEWORK if you want the full breakdown."

---

## Angle 4: VISION / ASPIRATIONAL

**Formula:** Paint future picture of what's possible

**Energy:** "This is what I'm building toward"

**Frequency:** 1-2x per week

**Best days:** Friday (end of week hope), Sunday (reset energy)

**Structure:**
1. Paint the future state
2. Contrast to current state (implicit)
3. Name what's required to get there
4. Conviction or call to join

**Examples:**
- "I created a legacy and still have time for leisurely lunch"
- "Your business runs. You decide whether to show up."
- "Time-rich CEO: the revolution nobody's talking about"
- "What happens when your intelligence finally works for you"
- "Scale that doesn't require you"

**Why it lands:**
Aspiration + permission. People want this future. This post says it's possible.

**Very high engagement** (saves, shares, DMs)

**CTA approach:**
No CTA — vision stands alone. Or: "Comment FUTURE if..."

**Example CTA:**
(No CTA — the post is the call)

---

## Angle 5: SEMANTIC CLARIFICATION

**Formula:** Redefine a commonly misunderstood term

**Energy:** "I've been using this word wrong"

**Frequency:** 1x per month

**Best days:** Monday (start the week with clarity)

**Structure:**
1. Name the misunderstanding
2. Define what it actually means
3. Show why the distinction matters
4. Conviction

**Examples:**
- "Authenticity ≠ Vulnerability"
- "Category-of-one isn't uniqueness, it's clarity"
- "Scale ≠ more people"
- "Codification isn't templates"
- "Legacy ≠ viral"
- "Positioning ≠ visibility"

**Why it lands:**
Relief. People realize they've been using the word as their excuse. Now they can't.

**CTA approach:**
"DM [WORD] if..." or no CTA (clarification stands alone)

---

## Angle 6: ECONOMIC INSIGHT

**Formula:** Reveal the money/value hidden in the concept

**Energy:** "Oh, that's why this actually costs me"

**Frequency:** 1-2x per month

**Best days:** Thursday (practical day) or Friday (decision-making day)

**Structure:**
1. Name the hidden cost (time, money, opportunity)
2. Show where it comes from
3. Show what changes when you address it
4. Conviction or call to action

**Examples:**
- "Founder-dependency caps your valuation at X"
- "Every retraining cycle costs Y hours"
- "What 'your presence' actually costs your business"
- "The invisible tax of undocumented processes"
- "Why templates cost more than they save"

**Why it lands:**
Specificity. Money talks. When you name the cost, the problem becomes urgent and real.

**CTA approach:**
"DM [WORD] if..." or "Comment [WORD] if you've felt this cost"

**Example CTA:**
"DM VALUATION if you're ready to see what your business could be worth."

## Weekly Rotation Strategy

Rotate the angles to create varied content while staying on thesis.

### Recommended Weekly Pattern

**Week 1:**
- Monday: Provocative Reframe
- Wednesday: Question-Driven Diagnosis
- Friday: Vision/Aspirational

**Week 2:**
- Monday: Semantic Clarification
- Wednesday: Framework Reveal
- Friday: Economic Insight

**Week 3:**
- Monday: Provocative Reframe
- Wednesday: Vision/Aspirational
- Friday: Economic Insight

Then repeat the cycle.

**Rule:** No angle appears twice in the same week. This creates the perception of fresh content while maintaining consistency.

## CTA Strategy

Different angles demand different CTAs.

### High-Intent CTAs (Works for angles 1, 2, 3, 5, 6)
"DM [WORD] if..." — Permission-based, targets serious people

**Examples:**
- "DM DESIGN if you're ready to..."
- "DM CODIFY if..."
- "DM FRAMEWORK if..."
- "Comment CLARITY if..."

### No CTA (Works for angles 4, sometimes 3)
Vision and aspiration stand alone. No ask.

**Rule:** Vision posts rarely need CTAs. The aspiration IS the call.

### Mixed CTAs (Works for angle 2)
Diagnostic questions can have no CTA or permission-based CTA.

**Examples:**
- No CTA (question hangs)
- "Comment YES if..."
- "DM if you recognize this"

## What NOT to Do

❌ Don't ask "What do you think?" or "Tell me in comments"
❌ Don't make CTAs sound desperate
❌ Don't use urgency language in CTAs
❌ Don't repeat the same angle twice in a row
❌ Don't use the same CTA in every post
❌ Don't force a CTA into every post (some stand alone)

## Cross-Angle Combinations

Strong posts combine angles:

**Provocative Reframe + Economic Insight:**
"You think you have a delegation problem. You have a documentation problem. That's costing you X."

**Question-Driven + Vision:**
"How much of your business requires you? Now imagine none of it did."

**Framework Reveal + Semantic Clarification:**
"Here's what 'codification' actually means. Here's the framework it creates."

**Angle Selection in Batches**

When planning a batch of content:
1. Pick your angles for the week
2. Vary them (no repeats, no more than 2x the same angle weekly)
3. Match angles to content pillars where possible
4. Vary CTAs
5. Check that you're hitting different emotional doors
