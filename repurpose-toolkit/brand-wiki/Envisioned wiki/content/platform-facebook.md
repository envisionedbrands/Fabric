# Platform Playbook — Facebook

## Why Facebook Matters for Envisioned

Facebook is the community and nurture platform. While Instagram discovers and LinkedIn positions, Facebook builds ongoing relationship depth — particularly through Groups and Reels distribution to warm audiences. With up to 50% of the Feed now being recommended content from accounts you don't follow, there's a genuine discovery opportunity here that many high-ticket brands are ignoring.

## How the Algorithm Works (2026)

Facebook uses a four-step ranking process:

1. **Inventory** — all available content from friends, groups, pages, and recommended sources
2. **Signals** — engagement history, content type, recency, relationship strength
3. **Predictions** — likelihood you'll engage with this specific piece of content
4. **Relevance Score** — final ranking that determines feed position

### Most Powerful Engagement Signals (2026)
- **Saves and DM shares** — more valuable than reactions or comments
- **Content that gets bookmarked or sent to a friend** gets significantly more distribution
- **First 6 hours** after posting is the algorithm's "testing period" — interaction density in this window is critical
- **Recency** — the algorithm still prioritizes fresh content

### The 50% Recommended Content Shift
Up to half of your Feed is now content from accounts you don't follow. This means your content can reach people who've never heard of you — IF the engagement signals are strong enough in the testing window.

## Content Format Strategy

### Reels (Primary Growth Format)
- Every video uploaded to Facebook is now automatically classified as a Reel
- Short-form video is the primary format the algorithm uses for distribution
- Structure: **Hook** (problem/promise in 3 seconds) → **Proof** (result/before-after) → **Steps** (2-5 quick steps) → **CTA** (one action)
- Best format for reaching new, broad audiences

### Recommended Weekly Cadence
| Format | Frequency | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Reels | 3x/week | Discovery and reach |
| Feed posts | 2x/week | Authority and engagement |
| Promotional posts | 1x/week | Offers and conversion |

This 3-2-1 cadence feeds the algorithm what it wants (video and engagement) while still driving business results.

### The 80/20 Rule
- 80% value-driven content (education, philosophy, stories, insight)
- 20% promotional content (offers, CTAs, launches)

## Community Strategy (Groups)

Facebook Groups are the strongest retention and conversion tool on the platform:

- More personalized and direct interaction with community
- Fosters belonging — members connect with each other, not just with you
- Exclusive content and offers enhance loyalty
- Weekly prompts, live videos, helpful tips build trust and long-term relationships
- Groups create a layer of community that algorithm changes can't touch

### Group Strategy for Envisioned
- Consider a private group for Envisioned clients/members — exclusive content, early access, direct support
- Weekly live Q&A or "office hours" format
- Community-led conversations reduce founder dependency (the exact problem you solve for clients)

## Engagement Best Practices

- **Respond within 4-8 hours** to all comments, DMs, and questions — speed signals active community to the algorithm
- **UGC and testimonials** outperform polished brand content — authentic customer reviews and real stories > studio production
- **Comment engagement** signals to the algorithm that your page fosters genuine interaction, improving all content ranking
- **Live video** sends notifications and gets algorithmic priority — use for launches, Q&As, and behind-the-scenes

## What Works on Facebook (Maria-Inés Application)

- **Reels repurposed from Instagram** — same content, different distribution. Facebook Reels reach different audiences than IG Reels.
- **Community over broadcast.** Facebook rewards relationship-dense interactions. The [[buyer-journey]] silent lurker → community member → client path works well in Groups.
- **Long-form video for philosophical content.** Facebook's audience has higher tolerance for longer content than Instagram — the 3-5 minute AI philosophy videos can live here uncut.
- **Testimonial content.** [[case-studies]] and client transformation stories perform exceptionally well — UGC signals outperform brand-produced content.

## What to Avoid

- Posting external links without context (algorithm deprioritizes link-only posts)
- Ignoring comments and DMs (kills algorithmic ranking)
- Over-produced, "corporate" content (authenticity wins)
- Treating Facebook as secondary to Instagram — the recommended content shift makes it a legitimate discovery platform
- Posting without a first-6-hours engagement strategy

## Envisioned-Specific Tactics

1. **Cross-post Reels from Instagram** — different audience, same content investment. Facebook's distribution is additive.
2. **Use Groups as the "inner circle" layer** — this maps to the [[buyer-journey]] implementation stage where clients need community.
3. **Go live for launches** — Facebook Live notifications bypass the algorithm entirely. Use for [[Envisioned Brand Experience]] launches, Power Portrait reveals, etc.
4. **Facebook = the nurture layer** — IG discovers, LinkedIn positions, Substack converts intellectually, Facebook nurtures the relationship over time.
5. **Respond to every comment** — build the engagement density signal that tells the algorithm this page is worth distributing.

## Key Metrics to Track

- Save and share rate per post (the #1 algorithm signals)
- Engagement within first 6 hours (testing window performance)
- Reel views vs. follower count (discovery ratio)
- Group member activity rate
- Comment response time
