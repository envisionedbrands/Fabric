# Emotional Hooks — 6 Narrative Triggers

Each hook triggers a specific emotion and has a specific deployment context. These are the reusable narrative levers in all Envisioned content.

---

## Hook 1: The Reconstruction Tax

**Emotion Triggered:** Frustration + Recognition (the "Oh... that's me" moment)

**The Story:**
> "Every time you create a proposal, a piece of content, a client strategy — you start from scratch. Not because the previous version didn't exist. Because it's buried somewhere you can't find, in a format you can't use, dependent on a version of your energy you can't guarantee."

**The Insight:**
You're paying a silent tax every week. Not in money (though that too). In energy. In repeated labor. In the friction between what you know and what you can access.

**Quantification (Makes It Real):**
€9,750/year rebuilding your own genius. (6 hours/month × 4 weeks × €40/hour)

The number makes the invisible visible. It's not vague frustration anymore — it's a line item.

**Deployment Context:**
- Top-of-funnel (Instagram, early content)
- Sales pages (value proposition section)
- First encounter (captures attention + recognition simultaneously)
- Names something everyone experiences but nobody has language for

---

## Hook 2: The Mirror

**Emotion Triggered:** Discomfort + Curiosity (the "wait, what?" moment)

**The Story:**
> "AI isn't disappointing you. It's showing you. It's reflecting back exactly how much of your work is built on effort versus essence."

**The Insight:**
AI forces a reckoning. When you can automate away the effort, what remains? If the answer is "nothing," you built a job, not a business.

**Why It Lands:**
- Reframes failure as wisdom (AI isn't broken, it's honest)
- Creates productive discomfort (makes people lean in, not away)
- Separates serious prospects from casual followers

**Deployment Context:**
- Mid-funnel (Substack, deeper content)
- Objection handling ("But I like working hard")
- When someone is ready to go deeper
- Acts as a fork — lean in or leave

## Hook 3: The Valuation Cap

**Emotion Triggered:** Ambition + Urgency (the "I need to fix this" moment)

**The Story:**
> "Your business is worth what you can generate, not what it can generate. You've built a high-paying consulting gig, not a scalable business."

**The Reframe:**
You might be making great money. That's not the same as having a valuable business. One is dependent on you. The other isn't.

**Why It Lands:**
- Challenges the assumption that revenue = success
- Points to a ceiling nobody likes talking about
- Creates urgency (the longer you wait, the more you're leaving on the table)

**Deployment Context:**
- Sales conversations (DMs, calls)
- When someone says "I'm doing fine"
- ICP challenge phase (when they need the bigger vision)
- High-end offer positioning (worth premium investment)

**Variant for Different Audiences:**
- For 6-figure founders: "Your next million is trapped behind your personal bandwidth."
- For 7-figure founders: "You've hit the ceiling that your genius alone can build."

## Hook 4: The Long Lunch

**Emotion Triggered:** Desire + Aspiration (the "I want that" moment)

**The Story:**
> "She's not escaping the business. The business runs so she can be here. In Paris. At a table. Having the kind of lunch that only happens when the work is done — not paused, not managed from your phone. Done."

**The Visual:**
Golden hour light. A table. Pasta. A conversation with someone who matters. Time that belongs to her.

**Why It Lands:**
- Shows the actual outcome (not revenue, not growth, but **presence**)
- Makes legacy living concrete and visual
- Creates belonging ("I want to be the kind of person who does this")

**Deployment Context:**
- Visual content (Instagram, Codified in the City imagery)
- Lifestyle positioning
- The final emotional image that makes someone DM
- [[codify-in-the-city]] marketing (the lunch is literally part of the experience)

**Variants:**
- The solo trip (proof of freedom)
- The grocery line (ordinary proof)
- The empty calendar (space as luxury)

## Hook 5: The Nervous System

**Emotion Triggered:** Exhaustion + Relief (the "finally someone said it" moment)

**The Story:**
> "Your business runs on your nervous system. Not on systems. On yours. Which means when your energy dips, when life intervenes, when your body says 'not today' — everything stalls."

**The Insight:**
You've optimized the business around your capacity. When capacity drops (illness, family, burnout), the business collapses.

**Why It Lands:**
- Meets people in fatigue
- Gives a structural diagnosis to a personal problem
- Points to a solution (build infrastructure, not more energy management)

**Deployment Context:**
- Anywhere in the "I'm exhausted but can't stop" zone
- Content aimed at burnt-out founders
- Objection handling ("I just need better time management")
- Early awareness phase (names the problem before proposing the solution)

---

## Hook 6: The Venezuelan Lens

**Emotion Triggered:** Authority + Gravity (the "she's actually serious" moment)

**The Story:**
> "I grew up watching systems fail. Not metaphorically. I'm Venezuelan. I watched an entire country's infrastructure collapse in real time because it was all built around one centralized authority."

**The Insight:**
Single points of failure don't just affect countries. They affect businesses. Founder-dependent businesses have the same architectural flaw.

**Why It Lands:**
- Uses lived experience as authority (no credentials needed)
- Adds weight and gravity to the philosophy
- Explains why she's obsessed with codification and distribution
- Creates differentiation (nobody else in this space has this lens)

**Deployment Context:**
- Origin story moments
- Podcast interviews
- Keynotes
- Substack deepdives
- When establishing why this matters beyond business tactics

## Polarizing Hooks Ranked by Edge

From least to most aggressive. Use based on audience readiness.

| Edge Level | Hook | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **1 (Gentle)** | "Inspiration is not a business model" | Social media (broad audience) |
| **2 (Medium)** | "Your nervous system is not a business strategy" | Content (awareness phase) |
| **3 (Sharp)** | "Your expertise isn't intangible. It's just undocumented." | Sales (consideration phase) |
| **4 (Most)** | "If your business only works when you're present, it's not an asset. It's a hostage situation." | High-end offers (decision phase) |

**Rule:** Start gentle, escalate as they move closer to decision. Early audience sees edge-1. Late-stage prospects see edge-4.

---

## Hook Rotation System (From 6-Door Framework)

Each hook represents a different emotional door into the ONE thesis: *"AI isn't a productivity hack—it's a mirror that forces you to decide who you are when effort no longer proves worth."*

In any batch of 6 posts, use all 6 hooks in different content pieces. Never repeat the same hook twice in a row (boring + ineffective).

---

## Deployment Checklist

Before using any hook:

✅ **Matches the audience stage** (awareness vs. consideration vs. decision)
✅ **Triggered by something real** (story, moment, observation)
✅ **Leads to a question, not an answer** (hook = opening, not closing)
✅ **Followed by reframe or diagnosis** (don't just complain, illuminate)
✅ **Moves toward the offer** (every hook should point somewhere)
✅ **Feels true to Maria-Inés** (sounds like her, not generic)
