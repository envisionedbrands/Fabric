# How to Use This Wiki

> This wiki is the shared brain between you, Liberty (Telegram), and Envisioned AI (Cowork). Everything below is something you can ask either of us — and we'll pull from the wiki to do it right.

---

## Voice & Writing

**1. "Write me an Instagram caption about [topic]. Use my actual voice from the wiki, not generic AI voice."**
We cross-reference [[voice-guide]], [[glossary]], [[forbidden-phrases]], and your 1,736 past captions in the Content vault to write like you — not like a brand strategist template.

**2. "Here's a voice note transcript. Turn it into a post."**
Content Engine skill + wiki = your [[Story-Weave Method]], [[6 Doors]] rotation, and [[mariela-abc-framework]] applied to raw material. You talk, we structure.

**3. "Pull 5 of my best past captions about [topic] and show me the patterns."**
We search the Content vault (1,736 pieces across 24 topic tags), surface what worked, and tell you WHY using [[content-pillars]] and [[content-angles]].

**4. "Rewrite this draft. It doesn't sound like me."**
We compare against [[voice-guide]], [[personality-architecture]], [[nasameter]], and 866 real full-length captions to fix it. If it fails the NASAmeter, we'll tell you.

**5. "Write a week of content using the 6-door rotation."**
We pull a different door for each post from [[content-angles]], open with different stories from [[story-bank]], apply [[mariela-abc-framework]] ratios, and keep every post pointed at the ONE message.

## Strategy & Positioning

**6. "I'm thinking about launching [new offer]. Pressure-test it against my brand positioning."**
We check it against [[competitive-positioning]], [[pricing-philosophy]], [[offer-architecture]], and [[ideal-client-profile]]. Does it fit the ladder? Does it serve the right buyer stage?

**7. "What's the gap between what I talk about and what I sell?"**
We cross-reference your 24 content topics (from the Content vault) against [[offer-architecture]] and show where you're creating demand for things you don't offer — or selling things you never talk about.

**8. "Audit my last 30 posts for voice drift."**
We compare recent content against [[voice-guide]], [[forbidden-phrases]], and [[personality-architecture]] and flag where you're slipping into generic territory, softening language, or losing the confrontational edge.

**9. "Build me a content calendar for the next 2 weeks around [launch]."**
We use [[story-bank]], [[6 Doors]] rotation, [[mariela-abc-framework]] (50/30/20), and the platform playbooks ([[platform-instagram]], [[platform-substack]], [[platform-linkedin]], [[platform-facebook]]) to plan it across every channel.

**10. "What topics am I overindexing on? What am I ignoring?"**
We analyze topic distribution across all 1,250 tagged pieces in the Content vault and surface the imbalance. Then we check it against [[content-pillars]] and the [[6 Doors]] to tell you what's missing.

## Client & Sales

**11. "Write a DM response to someone who said 'I want Milan.'"**
We use [[buyer-journey]] (where they are emotionally), [[audience-language-bank]] (words that resonate), and [[objection-architecture]] to craft a response that meets them exactly where they are.

**12. "Draft a proposal for [client name/type]."**
The proposal skill pulls from [[offer-architecture]], [[competitive-positioning]], [[pricing-philosophy]], and [[brand-narrative-architecture]] to build something that sounds like you, not like a template.

**13. "Someone asked about pricing. Write me a voice note script for how to talk about it."**
We reference [[pricing-philosophy]] — price = what the founder gets BACK, not what's delivered. Cognitive load pricing. The emotional sequence. Written in your spoken voice, not caption voice.

**14. "Build me a case study from this client's results."**
We use the template in [[case-studies]] and your storytelling structure from [[Story-Weave Method]]. Moment → tension → transformation → conviction. Real proof, not testimonial fluff.

## Content Repurposing

**15. "Take my best 10 Instagram captions and turn them into LinkedIn posts."**
We adapt format and framing for B2B using [[platform-linkedin]] best practices (Depth Score, no external links, PDF carousels) without losing your voice from [[voice-guide]].

**16. "Find my best-performing content about [topic] and repurpose it into a Substack article."**
We mine the Content vault, weave multiple captions into long-form using [[Story-Weave Method]], and format for [[platform-substack]] — one essay, one thesis, one door.

**17. "Turn this Substack post into 5 social media posts."**
We break long-form into social pieces using [[content-angles]], check [[mariela-abc-framework]] ratios, and adapt each piece for the right platform (IG carousel, LinkedIn text, FB Reel script).

**18. "Find content I've already written that I can repost with minor updates."**
We surface evergreen pieces from the Content vault that still hit — philosophical takes, identity pieces, and framework content that doesn't expire. Updated hooks, same thesis.

## Brand Intelligence

**19. "I just had a coaching call and my mentor said [X]. Here's what I took from it. Update my strategy."**
We add it to the wiki as a strategic input — NOT as content to parrot. Coaching intel is for strategy, not for captions. See [[content guardrails]] in the CLAUDE.md.

**20. "Extract my genius from this new client call transcript."**
Genius Extractor skill mines the transcript, cross-references against [[glossary]], [[personality-architecture]], and [[content-pillars]]. Finds patterns you can't see because they're automatic to you.

**21. "Compare my positioning to [competitor]. Where am I winning? Where am I exposed?"**
We use [[competitive-positioning]] (4 market types, 7 differentiators) + web research to show where you own the space and where someone else is closing the gap.

**22. "I said something on a call today that I want codified. Here it is: [quote]. Add it to my wiki."**
We slot it into the right article — [[glossary]] if it's a new term, [[story-bank]] if it's a new story, [[content-angles]] if it's a new reframe. Proper sourcing, proper confidence score.

## Systems & Operations

**23. "Run a wiki lint — what's outdated, what needs updating?"**
We check confidence scores, dates, and flag anything stale per [[wiki-maintenance]] rules. 90+ days unchanged = review. Pricing changes = immediate update. You contradicted something = immediate fix.

**24. "I changed my pricing / dropped an offer / added a new one. Update everything."**
We cascade the change across every wiki article that references it — [[offer-architecture]], [[pricing-philosophy]], [[buyer-journey]], [[substack-paid-strategy]], and any content that mentions the old offer.

**25. "Generate image prompts for my next 5 posts."**
Visual Intelligence skill + [[brand-strategy]] (color palette, visual archetype) + the specific post content = on-brand Midjourney/Krea/Flux prompts that match your Sophisticated 60% + Rustic 40% visual identity.

## How It All Connects

```
You speak (voice note / text / call)
        ↓
Liberty captures it (Telegram → Obsidian)
        ↓
Envisioned AI builds from it (wiki + Content vault + skills)
        ↓
Output: posts, strategies, proposals, case studies, image prompts
        ↓
Everything feeds back into the wiki (new stories, updated offers, refined voice)
```

The wiki isn't a document you read. It's the operating system underneath every request. The more it knows, the less you have to explain, and the closer the output gets to sounding like it came from you — because it did.
