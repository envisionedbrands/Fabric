# Forbidden Phrases — What Never to Use

These phrases are banned from all Envisioned content. They're either too generic, too soft, too "every other brand," or fundamentally misaligned with María-Inés's voice.

## Generic Softening (Never Use These)

These neutralize conviction and belong to voices that are trying to be liked, not understood.

❌ "I think"
❌ "Maybe"
❌ "Just"
❌ "Sort of"
❌ "Kind of"
❌ "Actually" (at sentence start)
❌ "Honestly"
❌ "To be honest"
❌ "I'm being vulnerable when I say..."
❌ "Let me be real with you"

**Why:** They hedge. María-Inés doesn't hedge. She states. If she's uncertain, she says why. She doesn't soften it.

## Inspiration-as-Strategy (Never Use These)

These are motivational speaker language. They announce enthusiasm instead of demonstrating truth.

❌ "Imagine waking up to..."
❌ "What if you could..."
❌ "Picture this..."
❌ "You deserve..."
❌ "You've got this!"
❌ "Don't give up!"
❌ "Believe in yourself!"
❌ "You're capable of more!"
❌ "It's time to finally..."
❌ "Join me on a journey..."
❌ "You're going to transform your..."
❌ "Unlock your potential"

**Why:** They're emotional manipulation. They assume the reader needs encouragement to feel capable. María-Inés assumes intelligence. She diagnoses. She shows the thing that's actually true.

## Urgency Theater (Never Use These)

Artificial scarcity. Countdown timers. FOMO tactics. All banned.

❌ "Don't miss out!"
❌ "Limited spots available"
❌ "This won't last long"
❌ "Grab yours before..."
❌ "Act now"
❌ "Only X people can..."
❌ "This is closing soon"
❌ "Last chance to..."

**Why:** She doesn't create artificial urgency. Real urgency (actual scarcity) gets stated. Manufactured urgency signals she doesn't trust the work to speak for itself.

## Engagement Bait (Never Use These)

These are for follower farming, not for building actual relationships.

❌ "What do you think?" (at post end)
❌ "Tell me in the comments"
❌ "Drop a comment below"
❌ "Tag someone who..."
❌ "Double tap if..."
❌ "Share this if..."
❌ "Who else..."
❌ "Agree or disagree?"

**Why:** They ask for engagement instead of earning it. Her posts are either valuable enough to get comments organically, or they're not. She doesn't beg.

## Generic Business Wisdom (Never Use These)

These belong to every other business coach. They could be swapped into anyone's content.

❌ "Here's the thing..."
❌ "Here's what no one explains..."
❌ "Listen..."
❌ "The secret is..."
❌ "Proven system"
❌ "Follow these X steps"
❌ "Success formula"
❌ "Best practices"
❌ "Game-changer"
❌ "Level up"
❌ "Shift your mindset"
❌ "Think differently"
❌ "Work smarter, not harder"
❌ "Scale your impact"

**Why:** They're interchangeable. Nobody should be able to insert another founder's name and have the post still work. Her voice is specific to her philosophy and experience.

## Tech Bro Language (Never Use These)

Cold, clinical jargon without philosophy. Assumes intelligence as substitute for clarity.

❌ "Optimize"
❌ "Leverage"
❌ "Synergize"
❌ "Disrupt"
❌ "Blockchain"
❌ "Growth hacking"
❌ "Viral growth"
❌ "Scalability metrics"
❌ "Burn rate optimization"

**Why:** She critiques tech hype. She doesn't participate in it. She uses tech as a means to philosophy, not as the philosophy itself.

## Emoji Overload (Never Use These)

Emojis as enthusiasm markers. Lazy punctuation.

❌ 🎉 (celebration emoji)
❌ 🚀 (rocket emoji, except rarely and ironically)
❌ ✨ (sparkle emoji)
❌ 💯 (perfect emoji)
❌ 😍 (love emoji)
❌ 🔥 (fire emoji, except once per 10 posts as emphasis)

**Rule:** Emojis are rare. When used, they're structural (💋 as period on truth statements) or ironic, never enthusiastic. Maximum 1 per post, usually 0.

## Announced Emotion (Never Use These)

When she says she's feeling something, it replaces showing it. These all announce feeling instead of demonstrating.

❌ "I'm so excited to share..."
❌ "I'm still buzzing about..."
❌ "I'm obsessed with..."
❌ "I'm passionate about..."
❌ "I'm honored to..."
❌ "I'm thrilled to..."

**Why:** If the thing is good, the reader will feel the energy. If she has to announce it, the thing isn't actually good enough.

## Apology & Overexplanation (Never Use These)

These soften authority. They apologize for existing.

❌ "I'm sorry if..."
❌ "I apologize for..."
❌ "I know this might be controversial..."
❌ "This might not be for everyone..."
❌ "I could be wrong, but..."
❌ "Feel free to disagree..."
❌ "No pressure, but..."

**Why:** She's not sorry. She has a point. If it's true, it stands. If someone disagrees, they disagree. She doesn't preemptively apologize for having convictions.

## Hand-Holding Explanations (Never Use These)

These assume the reader is dumb. They explain when they should orient.

❌ "Let me explain..."
❌ "Think about it this way..."
❌ "What I mean is..."
❌ "In other words..."
❌ "To clarify..."
❌ "Here's how it works..."
❌ "It's simple, really..."

**Why:** She orients. She doesn't explain. The reader is intelligent. She assumes that.

## Guru Language (Never Use These)

Mystical without practical. Inspiration dressed as wisdom.

❌ "The universe wants..."
❌ "Manifest your..."
❌ "Align with your..."
❌ "Your energy..."
❌ "The law of..."
❌ "Send this energy..."
❌ "Believe and..."

**Why:** She's not a spiritual guru. She's a systems strategist who worked in international development. Her philosophy is grounded in what actually breaks under pressure.

## Hashtag Farming (Never Use These)

Hashtags buried in content. Hashtag stacking. Forced keyword insertion.

❌ #blessed
❌ #hustle
❌ #grind
❌ #entrepreneur
❌ #mindset
❌ #transformation
❌ #goals
❌ #success

**Rule:** Hashtags are used strategically in first comment. Not in post. They're for reach, not aesthetic. Focus on #intentionalceo, #ethicalai, #aiforfounders.

## Comparison Language (Never Use These)

These position her against others. She leads forward, not against.

❌ "Unlike other..."
❌ "Better than..."
❌ "We're different because..."
❌ "They don't understand..."
❌ "Most people..."

**Why:** It's insecure positioning. She doesn't need to tear down others to be clear about what she does. She states her philosophy and lets it stand.

## Call-Out Language (Never Use These)

These shame. She diagnoses, she doesn't shame.

❌ "If you're still..."
❌ "Anyone who believes..."
❌ "People who don't..."
❌ "Stop acting like..."

**Why:** She critiques systems and patterns, not people. Even when she's sharp, she's precise about what's broken, not about who's broken.

## What She Actually Sounds Like

✅ "This isn't X. It's Y."
✅ "Here's what's actually happening."
✅ "The problem is structural, not personal."
✅ "This happens because of [specific reason]."
✅ "You build for X because [philosophy]."
✅ "Three things become inevitable."
✅ Short. Medium. Short. Long.
✅ Diagnostic accuracy.
✅ No apology.
✅ Conviction.

## The One Rule

If you catch yourself using any forbidden phrase, stop. Ask: "Would María-Inés say this?"

If the answer is "no," replace it with what she would actually say.

If you don't know what she'd say, study the [[voice-guide]]. Read 20 of her best posts. Notice what she does instead.

The voice isn't limiting. It's freeing. It's the set of constraints that make the work undeniable.
