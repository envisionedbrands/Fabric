# Offer Architecture

The complete Envisioned Brands ecosystem. One thesis, one methodology, three containers. Everything stacks. Nothing duplicates. Every rung is complete in itself.

## The Ladder

```
Cowork Lab (€27-97)
    ↓
Genius Map (€197-297)
    ↓
Decision Detox Sprint (€297-497)
    ↓
Workflow Extraction (€2,500-3,500)
    ↓
Capacity Mapping™ (€3,500)
    ↓
The Atelier: Foundation (€3,500)
    ↓
The Atelier: Build (€5,500)
    ↓
Time Rich CEO (€7,500-10,000)
    ↓
Intentional CEO Sprint + Codified in the City (€7,497)
```

## Understanding the Ladder

Each tier serves a specific role:

**Entry Tier (€27-297):** Permission to explore. Low friction, high curiosity. "See what's possible" without commitment.

**Diagnostic Tier (€297-3,500):** Founder knows there's a problem, not yet the architecture. Decision Detox → Workflow Extraction → Capacity Mapping are three entry points to the same understanding: what needs to change structurally.

**Implementation Tier (€3,500-5,500):** The Atelier exists. In Paris. Physical, bespoke. Proof point that business can run while you're present somewhere beautiful.

**Transformation Tier (€7,500-10,000):** Time Rich CEO. 90-day reconstruction. Founder-dependency eliminated structurally.

**Proof + Vision Tier (€7,497):** Intentional CEO Sprint + Codified in the City. Not an upsell. A different entry to same transformation, but with proof-of-concept embedded.

## Bridge Language (All Touchpoints)

> "If you decide to go further, everything you've invested applies forward. Work is never wasted. It's foundation."

This is the commitment architecture. No rung feels abandoned. Stop at any point with something real. Each rung makes the next deeper, faster, more powerful.

## What Envisioned Actually Sells

(Not what it appears to sell)

- Choice and sustainability, not more work
- Business that holds you when you can't hold it
- The moment expertise exists outside you (not stuck in your head)
- Liberation from being the nervous system
- Permission to be a CEO instead of a founder-operator

## Design Principles

1. **Modularity:** Each offer is complete. Can stop. Can scale. Can go deeper.
2. **Ascending specificity:** Broader offerings early (Cowork Lab), more targeted later (Capacity Mapping™).
3. **Bridge integrity:** Everything applies forward. No sunk cost. No waste.
4. **Proof embedded:** Every tier contains proof of next tier. Time Rich CEO client builds roadmap during Capacity Mapping™. The Atelier Foundation becomes The Build. Nothing is theoretical.
