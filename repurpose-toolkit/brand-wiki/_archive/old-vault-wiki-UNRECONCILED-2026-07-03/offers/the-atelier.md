# The Atelier

Two tiers, both in-person in Paris private studio. Bespoke business architecture. Su Misura (made to measure).

## The Atelier: Foundation | €3,500 | Half-day (4 hours)

**The Architecture:**

### 1. Business Archaeology (60-75 min)
Deep scan of business as it exists now. Not how it should exist. How it actually exists.

What's examined:
- Content ecosystem (what you've written, what's alive)
- Offer structure (what you sell, how it's positioned, pricing logic)
- Client profile (who's buying, why they buy, what they experience)
- Processes and workflows (where humans do work that shouldn't be human)
- Voice and positioning (how you communicate, consistency, authenticity)

Output: Complete inventory of business DNA.

### 2. Foundation Report (30 min)
Business scored across five dimensions:

1. **Identity Clarity** — How well is founder position known? Can client articulate why this founder matters?
2. **Offer Coherence** — Do offers stack logically? Do clients understand the ladder? Is positioning clear?
3. **Workflow Efficiency** — Where is founder trapped doing things infrastructure should do?
4. **Voice Consistency** — Does communication feel like one person? Or scattered across channels?
5. **Client Experience Architecture** — What does client actually experience from discovery → delivery → retention?

Each dimension scored. Gaps identified. No generic feedback. Specific to this business.

### 3. CLAUDE.md — Your AI Memory (30 min)
Custom AI instruction file for this founder.

What's inside:
- Complete business thesis in one place
- Offer ecosystem (the why, not just the what)
- Voice rules and signature patterns
- Client profile and what they actually want
- Decision frameworks and non-negotiables
- Stories that define the business
- Positioning and why it matters

Why it matters: AI that's trained on this file will remember your business, your methodology, your voice, your ethics. Won't default to generic consultant language. Becomes extension of your thinking.

### 4. Build Recommendation (45 min)
From the 50-tool library, which 5-8 systems would release the most capacity fastest for THIS business?

Not generic AI setup. Specific to founder's decision patterns, client experience, growth stage, and bandwidth.

Examples of systems:
- Client intake automation (captures information, creates structure, hands off to founder clean)
- Decision archive (captures decisions as made, so AI can reference them)
- Content processing pipeline (raw thinking → structured content → distributed)
- Client reminder system (touchpoints without founder effort)
- Approval workflows (keeps founder in loop without approval fatigue)

Phased into: Quick wins (deploy this week), core systems (deploy month 1), growth systems (deploy month 2-3).

### 5. Quick Win Build (45 min)
One system built on the spot. Live. Ready for tomorrow morning use.

Usually: client intake, decision capture, or simple automation that removes one decision loop.

Founder sees it work. Understands the pattern. Ready to implement others.

## Deliverables: Foundation

1. **Foundation Report** — Business scored, gaps identified, 5-8 system recommendations
2. **CLAUDE.md** — Custom AI memory file (lives in their docs, becomes permanent infrastructure)
3. **Build Recommendation** — Phased implementation roadmap (quick wins, core, growth)
4. **1 Working System** — Live, documented, tested
5. **Gap Closure Roadmap** — What comes next, in what order, why

## The Atelier: Build | €5,500 | Full day (7 hours)

**Everything from Foundation, PLUS:**

### 6. Deep Build Session (2.5h afternoon)
Two more complete systems deployed and live.

Afternoon is for building because:
- Morning archaeology established what needs building
- Founder has seen one pattern work
- Energy is focused (not divided)
- Systems can layer on each other

Two systems chosen from Build Recommendation. Usually: core systems (the high-leverage ones).

Examples:
- Content processing pipeline (thinking → structure → distribution)
- Decision archive + AI training (decisions captured, AI learns patterns)
- Client experience automation (pre-work, onboarding, check-ins)

### 7. Activation + Handoff (30 min)
Everything tested, documented, handed to founder with usage guides.

What's covered:
- How to activate each system
- When to use it
- How to evolve it
- How to train AI on it
- What to watch for (early warning signs system needs adjustment)
- 30-day follow-up call (built-in checkpoint)

## Positioning

"One day. Your business scanned, scored, and built from the inside out. Su Misura."

Not a retreat. Not a course. Not generic business coaching.

This is: Founder and architect, one studio, one day (or half-day), building bespoke infrastructure so founder becomes CEO instead of operator.

## Capacity & Logistics

**Capacity:** Maximum 2 Atelier sessions per Paris trip (preserves architectural integrity, prevents burnout)

**Payment Structure:**
- 50% deposit to book date
- 50% due on the day (cash or transfer)

**Location:** Private studio in Paris (address provided upon booking)

**Timezone:** Paris time. Plan your day accordingly.

**What to Bring:**
- Nothing required
- Optional: documented workflows or decision patterns you want to preserve
- Optional: screenshots of current systems that need improvement

**Pre-work:** 20-minute pre-call (1 week before) to identify highest-leverage systems to build

## The Su Misura Philosophy

Su Misura means "made to measure" in Italian. Not off-the-rack. Not template. Not generic.

Every Atelier is different because every founder is different. Same methodology. Entirely different output.

A founder building community experiences completely different systems than a founder building 1-on-1 services. A founder with a team needs different automation than a solo operator. A founder with €50K ARR has different leverage points than €500K ARR.

The method is universal. The application is specific.
