# Codify in the City

Codify in the City is NOT a product. It's a proof of concept made experiential. Lives inside Envisioned ecosystem but has its own gravitational pull.

---

## The Origin Story

Maria-Ines was in Milan. A founder saw her work. Didn't ask for details. Didn't ask for curriculum or pricing. Sent one DM:

> "I want Milan."

Not "tell me about your program." Not "is this right for me?"

Just: "I want Milan."

That DM is the entire brand DNA.

---

## What Codify in the City Actually Is

Not a retreat. (Retreats are escapes.)

Not a conference. (Conferences are information.)

Not a workshop. (Workshops are skill-building.)

**It's a proof of concept made visible.** The proof that your business runs so well you can be fully present somewhere beautiful and nothing falls apart.

---

## The Thesis

> "This isn't about going away. It's about your business being built so well it can run while you're present elsewhere. The long lunch isn't a break. It's proof."

---

## The Subversion (What Makes It Different)

Most high-ticket experiences ask you to abandon business to attend them.

Codify in the City does the opposite:

1. **Don't escape business.** Business runs while you're here.
2. **Don't just think.** Install (CEO Command Center, roadmap, infrastructure live in one day).
3. **Long lunch isn't break.** It IS methodology. It IS proof morning's work holds.
4. **Don't go home inspired.** Go home INSTALLED.

The experience proves the philosophy: business designed right = founder can be fully present.

## The Schedule

**9:00-12:30 | Deep Work / Installation**
- Business scanned (if Foundation tier)
- Systems built or roadmap installed
- Founder's year architected
- Everything from The Atelier, compressed

**12:30-14:30 | The Long Lunch**
Curated restaurant in the city. No phones. Intentional conversation. Food that requires presence.

Why long lunch matters:
- Proves morning's work holds without supervision
- CEO gets sustained presence (not fragmented)
- Community of founders in same room (isolation breaks)
- Luxury = signal that sustainability is worth investing in

**14:30-17:00 | Vision Lock + 12-Month Roadmap**
- Strategic direction finalized
- 12-month implementation roadmap built
- Proof-of-concept systems live and tested
- Next phase clarity established

**Evening (Optional)**
Community dinner, walks, exploration. Some founders come for work. Some use evening for integration.

## Cities & Their Teachings

Each city teaches something different. Each has been chosen for its specific philosophy:

### Paris | €4,500
**Art of Restraint**
"In Paris, luxury is subtraction. Everything unnecessary removed. Everything remaining essential."

Teaches: Codification isn't addition. It's revealing what matters, removing what distracts.

### Milan | €4,500
**Architecture of Identity**
"Milan is built. Intentional. Every building is positioned for specific reason. Nothing accidental."

Teaches: Business needs to be designed like architecture — structure determines experience.

### Amsterdam | €4,500
**Courage of Unconventional**
"Amsterdam didn't follow rules others made. Built their own way. Water management, urban design, values."

Teaches: Your business doesn't need to fit the template. It needs to be built for how you actually operate.

More cities coming as demand grows. Always chosen for specific philosophy, never for generic "nice place."

## The Long Lunch as Methodology

The long lunch is not a break. It's the core methodology made visible.

During morning: founder and architect install systems, build roadmaps, design infrastructure.

During lunch: business runs. No founder. Systems hold.

During afternoon: founder returns. Sees that nothing broke. Understands viscerally: business can run without constant presence.

This shift — from believing business needs you to knowing business works — is the entire transformation.

You don't learn this from a course. You experience it. You live it. You come back transformed.
