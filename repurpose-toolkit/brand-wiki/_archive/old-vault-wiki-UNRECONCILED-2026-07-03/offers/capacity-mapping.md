# Capacity Mapping™

€3,500 | Virtual | 10-14 days | Strategic diagnostic for structural redesign

Capacity Mapping™ is NOT implementation. It's architectural diagnosis. For founders with 5+ workflows where prioritization, not execution, is the bottleneck.

---

## The Problem It Solves

Founder knows business needs restructuring. Doesn't know where to start. Too many workflows, unclear which changes release capacity fastest. Risk of building wrong systems or building in wrong order.

Also: Founder has capable team but structural bottlenecks exist above the team level. Founder is the nervous system. Team is good. Architecture is the problem.

---

## The Capacity Mapping Process

### Week 1: Discovery & Mapping

**What Gets Examined:**
- All decision patterns (what decisions founder makes, when, why)
- All workflows (what processes exist, where founder touches them)
- Decision stalls (where decisions get stuck, delayed, reconsidered)
- Friction points (where founder keeps intervening because infrastructure isn't holding)
- Team capability gaps (what team can't do yet, where founder fills gap)
- Revenue impact (which workflows affect revenue most, which are overhead)
- Founder energy (which workflows energize founder, which drain)

**Output:**
Complete map of how founder's time and attention actually flows. Not how it should flow. How it actually flows.

### Week 2: Analysis & Sequencing

Deep analysis across three dimensions:

**1. Friction Map**
Visual of where decisions stall, where processes require founder intervention, where infrastructure is missing.

Usually shows: 2-3 core bottlenecks that create cascade of downstream friction.

Example pattern:
- Client intake requires founder because no system
- Founder spends 4h/week filtering applications manually
- This delays onboarding
- Delayed onboarding creates client confusion
- Client confusion requires extra support
- Extra support delays content creation

One bottleneck creates three problems. Fix bottleneck, three problems dissolve.

**2. Leverage Points (3-5 identified)**
Which 3-5 structural shifts release capacity fastest?

Not "optimize everything." Identify the 20% of changes that create 80% of capacity release.

Sequenced by:
- Impact (how much capacity does this release?)
- Dependencies (do other systems need this to work first?)
- Founder bandwidth (what can founder implement while running business?)
- Time horizon (what's quick win vs. 90-day project?)

Example leverage points:
- Move decision archive live (so AI can learn patterns, founder stops re-explaining)
- Automate client intake (founder stops reading 50 emails to accept 2 clients)
- Build content processing pipeline (thinking → structure → distribution without manual steps)

**3. Intelligence Audit**
What can be codified vs. must stay human? Where does AI preserve vs. distort?

Critical distinction:
- **Codifiable:** Decision patterns, frameworks, processes, voice, client experience architecture
- **Must stay human:** New relationship moments, high-stakes decisions, creative breakthroughs, ethical calls
- **Distorts if automated:** Client intimacy (if not positioned correctly), strategic direction, identity decisions

Audit identifies: What becomes infrastructure. What stays in founder's hands. How they interface.

## Deliverables

1. **Friction Map** — Visual representation of decision stalls and bottlenecks (document + optional visual)

2. **Leverage Points (3-5)** — Specific structural shifts ranked by:
   - Impact on founder capacity
   - Dependencies and sequencing
   - Estimated implementation time
   - Immediate vs. phased impact

3. **Intelligence Audit** — What can be codified without distortion. Where AI amplifies vs. replaces.

4. **Sequence** — Phased execution design:
   - Phase 1 (30 days): Quick wins
   - Phase 2 (60 days): Core systems
   - Phase 3 (90 days): Growth systems

5. **Ops Lead Brief** (if applicable) — If founder has team, brief for who implements what and when

## Positioning

**"The business is ready. The architecture isn't."**

You don't have a people problem or tool problem. You have a structure problem. Your team is capable. Your clients are good. Your methodology is proven. The infrastructure holding them together is built on founder-effort, not design.

Capacity Mapping™ identifies what changes structurally so founder becomes director instead of operator.

## What Comes Next

Capacity Mapping™ leads to three possible paths:

### Path 1: The Atelier
"I know what needs to change. I want it built. Let's go to Paris."

Capacity Mapping™ provides the blueprint. The Atelier executes it. Foundation (€3,500) or Build (€5,500).

### Path 2: Workflow Extraction
"I know the leverage points. Let me start with one workflow removal."

Capacity Mapping™ prioritized them. Workflow Extraction executes one at a time.

### Path 3: Time Rich CEO
"I'm ready for full founder extraction. Let's do this comprehensive."

Capacity Mapping™ becomes Phase 1 of Time Rich CEO. Build recommendations become implementation roadmap.
