# Content Scorecard — Quality Assurance Framework

Before publishing, every post runs through this scorecard. Not a checklist. A lens for catching misalignment before it lands.

## The 5-Point Quality Check

### 1. ABC Ratio
Does this post hit the right category in the 50/30/20 distribution?

| Category | Target | Check |
|----------|--------|-------|
| A (Attention) | 50% | Is this shareable? Identity-level? Philosophical? Will people send this to a friend? |
| B (Building) | 30% | Does this build trust? Teach? Deepen understanding? |
| C (Conversion) | 20% | Does this position an offer? Move someone toward a decision? |

**Red flag:** Three B posts in a row. Audiences need the philosophical doors (A) to discover you.

### 2. 6-Door Rotation
Does this door appear twice in a row? Should it?

| Door | Frequency | Last Used |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Ethics/Identity | 1x per week | _____ |
| Myth-Busting | 1-2x per week | _____ |
| Case Study/Proof | 1x per week | _____ |
| Philosophy | 1x per week | _____ |
| Positioning | 1x per week | _____ |
| Movement/Vision | 1-2x per month | _____ |

**Rule:** No door repeats twice in a row. No door appears 3+ times in a week.

### 3. Opening Variety
Did the last 3 posts start differently?

**Types:**
- Story (moment, scene, memory)
- Question (diagnostic)
- Statement (bold claim)
- Framework reveal
- Contradiction (this vs. that)
- Data/insight

**Red flag:** Same opening type 2+ times in a row kills momentum.

### 4. Voice Consistency
Does the voice match these signatures?

✅ **Do this:**
- Sharp, direct language
- Philosophical binaries (THIS or THAT)
- One-sentence paragraphs for power
- CAPS for emphasis (not urgency)
- Conviction without hedging
- Story first, insight second

❌ **Don't do this:**
- "Listen..." or "Here's the thing..."
- Invitational language ("Consider..." "You might...")
- Apologetic tone
- Hedging ("I think maybe..." "You could possibly...")
- Psychology jargon
- Gentle, coaching energy

### 5. Platform Fit
Is this post optimized for WHERE it's being posted?

| Platform | Format Check | Engagement Signal | CTA Style |
|----------|-------------|------------------|-----------|
| Instagram | Hook in first line (3 sec) | Saves, DMs, Reshares | "DM WORD if..." |
| LinkedIn | Depth signal, document value | Comments, 5+ min read time | Strategic or none |
| Facebook | Community, Reels, engagement density | Saves, shares, first 6 hours | Video or community-driven |
| Substack | Nuance, length, intellectual depth | Paid conversion, replies | None or strategic |

### 6. Uniqueness Check
Has this exact angle/story/framework been posted in the last 60 days?

- Same story? No.
- Same framework? No.
- Same 6-door? Not back-to-back.
- Same emotional hook? Not within 2 weeks.

### 7. Content Guardrails
✅ This post serves the thesis
✅ Story is real (or explicitly framed as hypothetical)
✅ CAPS are selective and meaningful (not scattered)
✅ Opening hooks before explaining
✅ One clear conviction per post (no multiple asks)
✅ CTA is permission-based ("DM if..." not "DM me to learn more")
✅ Length matches platform (IG: 600-900 chars, LinkedIn: 1,200-1,800, Substack: 800-1,200)

❌ This post feels salesy
❌ Story is invented or heavily fictionalized
❌ CAPS are overused
❌ Explains before it hooks
❌ Multiple competing messages
❌ CTA is desperate or urgent
❌ Too long for the platform

## Pre-Publish Checklist

Before posting, run this 60-second check:

- [ ] Opens with a story, question, or bold statement (not an explanation)
- [ ] Contains one clear conviction
- [ ] ABC category matches target ratio
- [ ] 6-door rotation rule followed (no repeats)
- [ ] Opening type differs from last 2 posts
- [ ] Voice feels like María-Inés (sharp, direct, no hedging)
- [ ] Platform-specific formatting applied
- [ ] Story or framework hasn't been posted in 60 days
- [ ] CAPS are selective (1-3 per post max)
- [ ] CTA is permission-based or absent
- [ ] Length is right for platform

If any check fails, revise before posting.

## What This Scorecard Prevents

1. **Repetition fatigue** — same door, same story, same framework back-to-back
2. **Voice drift** — slipping into coaching language, hedging, or apologetic tone
3. **Algorithm misalignment** — posts that look good but don't have the right engagement density for the platform
4. **Ratio creep** — too much building content, not enough discovery content
5. **Conviction blur** — multiple messages competing in one post
6. **CTA desperation** — asks that feel needy instead of permission-based
