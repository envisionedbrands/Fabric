# Content Mechanics -- Borrowed Structure, Original Voice

> The MECHANICS are borrowed from Suzi Gray, Agus di Carlo, and Niche Arlow. The VOICE, THESIS, and POSITIONING are 100% Maria-Ines.

---

## Principle

Envisioned's content creation system is built from Maria-Ines's own best content plus structural mechanics borrowed from three creators. The distinction is critical: take the mechanic, never take the voice. The structures are scaffolding. The soul is Maria-Ines.

---

## From Suzi Gray (@iamsuzigray) -- Story-Weave Method

**What to borrow:**
- Story-first openings
- Poetic rhythm
- Quiet conviction

**How Maria-Ines uses it:** Suzi never opens with the insight. She opens with a life moment. The reader is inside a MOMENT before they know they are learning something.

Maria-Ines's version: "I connected a tool to my business this morning and my eyes welled up." / "I grew up watching systems fail. Not metaphorically. I'm Venezuelan." / "Yesterday I built a launch package from the grocery line."

**The rule:** The thesis EMERGES from the story. Never announce the thesis first and then illustrate it. Story, then tension, then reframe, then conviction.

**What to NEVER borrow:** Don't write in Suzi's gentle, invitational tone. Maria-Ines is more confrontational.

---

## From Agus di Carlo (@soy.agusdicarlo) -- 6-Door Rotation + Conviction Rhythm

**What to borrow:**
- 6-door rotation system (see [Content Pillars](../content/content-pillars.md))
- Offer-heavy distribution
- Repetition rule
- Conviction over tactics
- Permission-based CTAs

**How Maria-Ines uses it:** Short paragraphs. One sentence = one paragraph when making a point land. Build energy throughout. End with conviction, not a question. No hedging. Permission-based CTAs that filter for serious people: "Comment [WORD]" or "DM [WORD]."

**What to NEVER borrow:** Don't write in Spanish sales energy. Don't teach sales psychology.

---

## From Niche Arlow (@niche_arlow_wealth) -- Psychological Anchoring

**What to borrow:**
- Name the framework
- One-sentence paragraphs
- Vulnerability + authority in the same breath
- Keyword CTAs

**How Maria-Ines uses it:** Niche names the science (Zeigarnik Effect, Identity-Based Motivation) then translates to street-level language. Maria-Ines names the DESIGN PRINCIPLE or SYSTEMS CONCEPT underneath the story. Not psychology jargon -- AI architecture language:
- "Codified intelligence layer"
- "Operational infrastructure"
- "Perishable genius"
- "The interface between a founder and their business is now conversation"

Give the insight an intellectual anchor so it is not just a feeling -- it is a framework.

**What to NEVER borrow:** Don't use psychology jargon. Don't use circus energy. Don't lead with neuroscience.

## Signature Patterns (Maria-Ines's Own)

These are not borrowed -- they are native to Maria-Ines:

- **STORY-FIRST openings** -- Lead with a moment, a scene, something that happened
- **Triple repetition for rhythm** -- "You will... You will... You will..."
- **Bold typography for emphasis** -- Use **CAPS** for key concepts
- **Permission-based CTAs** -- "Comment [WORD]" or "DM [WORD]" to create psychological filters
- **Reframing formula** -- "This isn't [surface level]. It's [deeper truth]."
- **Confrontational but empathetic** -- Validate skepticism before dismantling it
