# Platform Playbook — LinkedIn

## Why LinkedIn Matters for Envisioned

LinkedIn is the B2B conversion platform. The [[ideal-client-profile]] — established founders, 6-7 figure businesses — lives here professionally. It's where positioning as the "Intentional CEO" thought leader reaches decision-makers who buy high-ticket services. Personal profiles generate 8x more engagement than company pages, which means Maria-Inés's personal brand IS the strategy.

## How the Algorithm Works (2026)

### The "Authenticity Update" (March 2026)
LinkedIn rolled out a major algorithmic shift targeting artificial engagement tactics. Advanced NLP now detects and penalizes engagement bait — phrases like "Comment below to get the link" are flagged by the system.

### The "Depth Score"
The Depth Score has officially replaced the simple "like" as LinkedIn's primary ranking signal. It measures:

1. **Saves** — the most important engagement signal in 2026. When someone saves your post, LinkedIn interprets it as lasting reference value and extends distribution.
2. **Dwell time** — how long someone spends reading/viewing your content
3. **Meaningful comments** — substantive replies, not "Great post!" (LinkedIn can now distinguish)
4. **Shares with added commentary** — not empty reshares

### Content Distribution Flow
Posts go through a quality filter → shown to a small % of your network → if engagement signals are strong in the first 60-90 minutes → expanded distribution → potential viral reach beyond network.

## Content Format Strategy

### Format Performance Rankings (2026 Data)
| Format | Engagement Rate | Best For |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| Document/PDF carousels | 6.60% (highest) | Educational content, frameworks, step-by-step |
| Native video | 5x more engagement than static | Thought leadership, behind-the-scenes |
| Text posts | Strong with good hooks | Stories, philosophical takes, positioning |
| Single images | Moderate | Quote cards, insight cards |
| Posts with external links | -40% reach | Avoid in post body — use first comment |

### Recommended Mix
- 50% carousel/video content
- 30% text posts (story-driven, philosophical)
- 20% document/image posts
- Keep 80% of posts as native content with NO external links

### Document Posts / PDF Carousels
- Display as swipeable slide decks — get extended dwell time
- 278% more engagement than video, 303% more than images, ~600% more than text
- Best posted Wednesday-Thursday, 9 AM-12 PM when audience has time to swipe through
- Design for mobile-first — large text, clear hierarchy, one idea per slide

### Video
- Native video receives 5x more engagement than static posts
- Algorithm specifically boosts educational and how-to content over promotional material
- Keep under 2 minutes for feed videos
- Captions/subtitles required — most LinkedIn browsing is sound-off

## Posting Cadence

### Frequency
- **Sweet spot: 3-5 posts per week** (data from 2M+ post analysis)
- Members who publish 2+ times per week see up to 5x more profile views
- **Never exceed 1 post per day** — accounts posting 2+ times daily see 40%+ drop in reach per post
- 6-10 posts/week provides stronger lift if you can maintain quality (+5,001 impressions per post)

### Best Times to Post
- **Tuesday through Thursday, 8 AM-11 AM** in your audience's timezone
- Peak slots: Wednesday 4 PM, Friday 3-4 PM
- Document posts specifically: Wednesday-Thursday, 9 AM-12 PM
- First 60-90 minutes of engagement determines distribution trajectory

## What Works on LinkedIn (Maria-Inés Application)

- **Real experiences over polished messaging.** Posts showing real experiences (including failures) consistently outperform corporate content. The [[Time-Rich CEO flop story]], the [[Venezuela story]] — these are LinkedIn gold.
- **Thought leadership with specificity.** Not "AI is changing business" but "I connected a tool to my business and my eyes welled up." Specific > general. Always.
- **Document carousels for frameworks.** The [[3 Layers of AI Users]], [[6 Doors]], [[Story-Weave Method]] — these are all carousel-ready frameworks that would get massive saves.
- **No engagement bait.** The algorithm now penalizes "Comment X to get Y." Use the permission-based CTA style: "DM me CODIFY" or "If this resonates, you know where to find me."
- **Authenticity as competitive advantage.** LinkedIn's algorithm now structurally rewards what Maria-Inés already does — write from lived experience with conviction.

## What to Avoid

- External links in post body (use first comment instead — 40% reach penalty)
- Engagement bait phrases ("Like if you agree," "Comment below for the link")
- Posting more than once per day
- Generic "AI thought leader" positioning — specificity wins
- Empty reshares without added commentary
- Company page posts as primary strategy (personal profile = 8x more engagement)

## Envisioned-Specific Tactics

1. **Repurpose Substack essays into LinkedIn text posts** — take the core REFRAME and CONVICTION from a Substack essay and reformat as a LinkedIn post. Different platform, same thesis.
2. **Turn [[glossary]] frameworks into PDF carousels** — "The 3 Layers of AI Users" as a 6-slide swipeable document would dominate saves.
3. **Cross-post philosophical depth** — LinkedIn audience wants the intellectual weight. The [[Ethics/Identity Door]] and [[Philosophy Door]] content performs especially well here.
4. **Profile optimization** — headline should read like a positioning statement, not a job title. "Building AI infrastructure that codifies founder brilliance" > "Brand Strategist."
5. **Comment strategy** — spend 15 min before and after posting engaging meaningfully on others' content. This builds network signals the algorithm uses for distribution.

## Key Metrics to Track

- Save rate (the #1 signal in 2026)
- Dwell time on document posts
- Profile views per week (benchmark: 5x increase at 2+ posts/week)
- Inbound DM conversations from posts
- Post impression trajectory (first 90 min engagement → extended reach)
