## Usage Notes

- **Pain agitation** hooks work best as video openers and DM-trigger posts
- **Contrarian** hooks drive the highest engagement through disagreement and shares
- **Curiosity** hooks pull lurkers out of silence -- strong for Story posts
- **Identity** hooks self-select the ideal client -- pair with Filter CTAs
- **Transformation** hooks work for launch content and case-study doors

When pairing hooks with the [6 Doors](../content/content-pillars.md):
- Pain agitation aligns with Myth-Busting and Case Study doors
- Contrarian aligns with Myth-Busting and Positioning doors
- Curiosity aligns with Philosophy and Movement doors
- Identity aligns with Ethics/Identity door
- Transformation aligns with Case Study and Movement doors
