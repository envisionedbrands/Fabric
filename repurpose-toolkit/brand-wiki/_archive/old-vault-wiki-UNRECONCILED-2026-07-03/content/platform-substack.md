# Platform Playbook — Substack

## Why Substack Matters for Envisioned

Substack is the long-form home base. It's where philosophical depth lives, where [[brand-narrative-architecture]] plays out in full, and where the [[ideal-client-profile]] audience self-selects. Unlike Instagram (attention economy) or LinkedIn (professional posturing), Substack rewards identity-led writing — exactly what Maria-Inés does best.

## How the Algorithm Works (2026)

Substack uses three discovery mechanisms:

**1. Notes Algorithm.** Functions as its own social platform. The algorithm surfaces Notes based on engagement signals from writers you interact with — restacking, replying, and liking create cross-audience visibility. When you engage with another writer's content, Substack interprets that as a shared-audience signal and shows your content to their followers.

**2. Recommendations Engine.** When other writers recommend your publication, their subscribers see you during signup. This is peer-validated growth — the highest-trust acquisition channel on the platform.

**3. In-App Discovery.** Substack reported 32 million new subscribers came from within the app itself over just 3 months (late 2025). Readers are finding writers inside Substack without external traffic.

## Posting Strategy

### Newsletter Frequency
- **1-2 long-form posts per week** is the sweet spot for a high-ticket brand
- Consistency matters more than volume — pick a cadence and hold it
- Free posts drive discovery; paid posts drive revenue and exclusivity

### Notes (The Growth Engine)
- **2-3 Notes daily**, ~20 minutes total investment
- Notes drove 70% of subscriber growth for top creators in 2025-2026
- Treat Notes like philosophical micro-posts, not throwaway tweets
- One main theme told through different stories from different angles
- Lead with YOUR story — not tips AI could generate

### Chat (Community)
- Host weekly Notes Boosts where writers cross-promote
- Run Q&As and actually talk with readers
- The relationships built in Chat convert to revenue and retention

### Live Video
- Sends notifications to your entire list — no algorithm involved
- Substack auto-creates clips from lives
- Highest-leverage growth tool on the platform right now

## Content Format That Works

| Format | Purpose | Frequency |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Long-form essay | Depth, authority, [[content-pillars]] | 1-2x/week |
| Notes | Discovery, daily presence, audience building | 2-3x/day |
| Chat threads | Community, retention, relationship depth | Daily engagement |
| Live video | Notification-driven reach, clip generation | 1-2x/month |
| Restacks | Cross-audience discovery, relationship signals | As relevant |

## What Works on Substack (Maria-Inés Application)

- **Identity-first writing.** Substack rewards recognizable voice. The [[voice-guide]] and [[Story-Weave Method]] are built for this platform.
- **Philosophical depth over tactical tips.** The audience here wants the WHY, not the HOW. This is where the "AI as identity reckoning" thesis plays in full.
- **Community over audience.** When you have genuine community, algorithm changes matter less — your people open emails, share work, and show up regardless.
- **Cross-platform repurposing.** Substack essays become the source material for Instagram carousels, LinkedIn posts, and content engine batches. Write once here, distribute everywhere.

## What to Avoid

- Generic advice that AI could generate — lead with lived experience
- Chasing trends instead of holding your thesis
- Treating Notes as an afterthought — it's where discovery happens
- Ignoring Chat — it's the retention and conversion layer

## Envisioned-Specific Tactics

1. **Use the [[6 Doors]] rotation** for essays — each Substack post enters through a different door
2. **Open with [[Story-Weave Method]]** — the long-form format lets you develop the MOMENT fully before the reframe
3. **Notes are for the [[glossary]] terms** — short punchy definitions of Codification, Perishable Genius, The Loop, etc. build intellectual authority
4. **Paid tier = implementation depth** — free posts establish philosophy, paid posts reveal the architecture underneath
5. **Every essay should end with a filter CTA** — DM "WORD" or "Reply to this email if..." — never beg for engagement

## Key Metrics to Track

- Subscriber growth rate (weekly)
- Open rate (benchmark: 40-60% for engaged lists)
- Notes engagement → subscriber conversion
- Paid conversion rate
- Chat participation rate
