# Heck Yes Framework

The Holly MacCue framework that Maria-Ines studies and applies to attract pre-sold, aligned clients.

This framework is about the psychology of invitation vs. convincing. Trust cannot be rushed. 95% of purchasing decisions are subconscious. The language of the subconscious is mental pictures.

---

## Core Philosophy

Selling doesn't require convincing. It requires showing.

You're not trying to convince people they need you. You're showing what becomes possible when they work with you.

The difference:
- **Convincing:** Founder works hard to change minds
- **Showing:** Founder creates invitation that changes minds automatically

---

## The 5 Things About Ideal Clients

Start here. In your ideal client's own words, what's true?

1. **How they describe themselves**
   - What words do they use?
   - What identity claim do they make?
   - Example: "I'm a founder who built something good but I'm trapped in it"

2. **Their most pressing problem**
   - Not generic problem. The problem that wakes them at 3am.
   - Example: "My business requires me. If I step back, it falls apart."

3. **Impact of that problem**
   - What does it cost them (time, money, energy, identity)?
   - Example: "I can't take a real vacation. I can't scale. I can't be present with my family."

4. **Their desired outcomes**
   - Not what they think they should want. What they actually want.
   - Example: "Business that runs when I'm not managing it. Being a director, not operator."

5. **Impact of desired outcome**
   - What becomes possible? Who do they become?
   - Example: "I get my time back. I become CEO. I can build something bigger. I have actual freedom."

## 6 Deal Breakers (Requirements for Success)

These six things must be present for founder to attract aligned clients and convert them.

### 1. HECK YES ALIGNMENT

**Definition:** Sweet spot between what you're obsessed with sharing and what ideal buyers actively ask for.

**Two Components:**

**Resonance:** Do you show you understand them?
- Do you speak their language?
- Do you name their problem in ways they recognize?
- Do they feel seen?

**Relevance:** Are you top-of-mind priority?
- Is this their burning question right now?
- Or something they think they should care about (but don't)?
- Can they not think about this? (You want: can't not think about it)

**Maria-Ines Example:**
Resonance: She names the identity problem ("You're building a high-paying job disguised as a business")
Relevance: She positions AI as identity mirror ("AI forces you to decide who you are when effort no longer proves worth")

### 2. HECK YES AUTHORITY

**Definition:** Connection to WHO you are + confidence in WHAT you do.

**Two Components:**

**Earned Authority:**
- You've demonstrated what you teach
- You have proof (case studies, results, before/after)
- You've shared thinking openly (content shows methodology)
- You've been transparent about journey (not hiding mistakes)

**Borrowed Authority:**
- Strategic collaborations (who endorses you?)
- Media mentions (who validates you?)
- Peer relationships (who stands beside you?)
- Community (who believes in you?)

**Maria-Ines Example:**
Earned: She's scaled her own business, extracted her own genius, lives what she teaches
Borrowed: Collaborations with Agus, Suzi, other strategists; Pentagon conversations; media presence

### 3. HECK YES ANTICIPATION

**Definition:** Pre-sale element. Create snackable appetizers that make people want the full buffet.

**How it works:**
- Your online presence = movie trailer for your offer
- Content shows what's inside
- Builds longing before first conversation
- By the time they reach out, they're pre-sold

**What creates anticipation:**
- Teachable frameworks (philosophy people can apply)
- Story moments (shows your thinking in action)
- Proof that it works (case studies, results)
- Clarity on what's inside (demystifies the offer)

**Maria-Ines Example:**
- Substack essays (frameworks, stories, philosophy)
- Video shorts (shows methodology in action)
- Client stories (before/after, anonymized or permitted)
- Offer architecture (clarity on ladder)

### 4. HECK YES ADVOCACY

**Definition:** Others like them achieving results they want.

**Components:**
- **Social proof:** Who else chose you? Who else sees results?
- **Testimonials:** Real words from real clients (not generic praise)
- **Before/after:** Can they see themselves in the before?
- **Case studies:** Detailed story of transformation

**Why it matters:**
People don't trust founders. They trust people like them who got results.

**Maria-Ines Example:**
- Client transformations (anonymized or permitted)
- Community testimony (what people say about her methodology)
- Visible results (what clients accomplished)
- Peer endorsements (who she's worked with, who endorses her)

### 5. HECK YES INVITATIONS

**Definition:** Search clarity (imagine what's inside) + search desirability (visualize the experience).

**Two Components:**

**Search Clarity:**
- Is it obvious what you do?
- Can they find you (SEO, keywords, positioning)?
- Can they articulate what you offer in their own words?

**Search Desirability:**
- Do they want to click?
- Does it look like something they want to experience?
- Is it positioned in a way that attracts them?

**Removes reasons to say no:**
- Clear next steps
- Low friction entry
- No hidden requirements
- Safe to explore

**Maria-Ines Example:**
Clear invitations for each tier (Cowork Lab is visible entry, easy to understand)
Desirable positioning (Paris intensives, Legacy Living, CEO elevation)
Clear next steps (Book call, DM "WORD", Join the community)

### 6. HECK YES FOLLOW-UP

**Definition:** "50% of sales lie in follow-up." Nurture as service, not pressure.

**What it includes:**
- Email sequences (not broadcast, curated)
- Touchpoints (regular, not harassing)
- Value-first (teach before asking)
- Seasonal outreach (holidays, milestones)
- Graduation paths (if not ready for main offer, what's next?)

**Why it matters:**
People don't buy on first touch. They buy when ready. Follow-up keeps you top-of-mind without being pushy.

**Maria-Ines Example:**
- Weekly newsletter (value + positioning)
- Email series (framework learning path)
- DM conversations (relationship building)
- Seasonal offers (holiday, New Year, summer)

## 4 Pillars of Magnetic Selling

How it all integrates:

### Pillar 1: Sales Foundations (Weeks 1-2)
Get your fundamentals right:
- Clear on your ideal client
- Clear on your offer (what's it for, who's it for, why it matters)
- Clear on your positioning (why you, not someone else)
- Clear on your promise (what do they become?)

### Pillar 2: Pre-Sale (Weeks 3-4)
Build anticipation before you pitch:
- Create content (frameworks, stories, proof)
- Build invitations (clear next steps, low friction)
- Establish authority (teach, show, prove)
- Create advocacy (testimonials, case studies)

### Pillar 3: The Sell (Weeks 5-6)
Have authentic sales conversations:
- Understand their situation (listen)
- Show how your work applies (specific to them)
- Let them sell themselves (they talk themselves into it)
- Present offer with confidence (no apology)

### Pillar 4: Follow-Up (Ongoing)
Nurture those not ready yet:
- Stay visible (regular touchpoints)
- Stay valuable (always teaching)
- Stay available (easy to re-engage)
- Stay patient (people buy when ready)

## Implementation Strategy (Maria-Ines Version)

This framework gets implemented through content, relationships, and strategic positioning.

**Month 1: Foundation**
- Clarify ideal client (5 things)
- Clarify offer (positioning, positioning, promise)
- Audit authority (what's earned, what's borrowed)

**Month 2: Pre-Sale Content**
- Create content framework (story-weave method)
- Teach 3-5 core frameworks (codification, freedom architecture, etc.)
- Share client stories (transformed + proof)

**Month 3: Invitations**
- Clarify all offers (ladder, pricing, positioning)
- Create funnels (Cowork Lab → Genius Map → deeper offers)
- Set up follow-up sequences

**Ongoing: Relationship Building**
- Weekly valuable content (Substack, social, email)
- Monthly community touchpoints
- Quarterly bigger initiatives (launches, intensives, proof experiences)

## The Underlying Truth

This framework works because it's based on psychology, not manipulation.

People don't want to be sold. They want to be shown.

They don't want pressure. They want permission.

They don't want generic advice. They want to see themselves in your story.

When you're aligned (showing what matters), authoritative (you've done it), anticipatory (they can imagine it), advocating for them (proof it works), inviting them in (easy next step), and following up (persistent but not pushy) —

They don't say "yes" because you convinced them.

They say "yes" because they can see themselves in the outcome.

That's the difference between selling and invitation.
