# Glossary — Proprietary Terms & Definitions

> Every term Maria-Ines uses that wouldn't mean anything out of context. The language IS the brand. If an AI session doesn't know these terms, it can't write as Envisioned.

---

## Identity & Philosophy

**Intentional CEO**
The identity shift Envisioned facilitates. A founder who has moved from "running a business" to "authoring a business." Not a title — an operating mode. The business runs on codified intelligence, not founder effort. See [[legacy-living-spin]].

**Legacy Living**
Maria-Ines's creative throughline: "I create a legacy and still have time for a leisurely lunch." Not work-life balance (which implies compromise). Legacy Living means the infrastructure IS the legacy, and the living proves it works. See [[legacy-living-spin]].

**The Codification Thesis**
The core reframe: AI isn't a productivity hack — it's a mirror that forces you to decide who you are when effort no longer proves worth. Three layers: Reframe (AI changes the question), Binary (codify or become obsolete), Reckoning (identity crisis when effort isn't required). See [[the-codification-thesis]].

**Freedom Architecture**
The opposite of productivity hacking. Designing business infrastructure so the founder's absence IS the proof that the system works. Not "work less" — "build so well that presence becomes optional." See [[freedom-architecture]].

**The Venezuelan Lens**
Maria-Ines's origin perspective: growing up watching an entire country's infrastructure collapse because it depended on centralized authority. This isn't a metaphor — it's lived experience that shapes everything. Single points of failure create systemic collapse. See [[founder-profile]].

**Evolution Revolution**
The movement Envisioned is building. Not incremental improvement — a fundamental shift in how founders relate to their businesses. AI as the catalyst, identity as the foundation, infrastructure as the proof.

## Methodology & Process

**Codification**
Extracting what lives in a founder's head — decision patterns, methodology, quality standards, instincts — and structuring it so AI systems and team members can operate from it. Not documenting SOPs. Documenting *thinking*.

**Identity Codification**
The first of [[positioning-statement#The Three Pillars|the three pillars]]. Making a founder's invisible methodology visible. The raw material that makes every other system possible.

**Founder-Dependency Elimination**
The second pillar. Structurally removing the bottleneck — not by replacing the founder, but by giving the business access to founder intelligence without requiring founder presence.

**AI Infrastructure**
The third pillar. Done-for-you operational layers built on codified intelligence. Not tool setup. Architecture. The systems that let a business respond to its founder's standards without needing her in the room.

**The Loop**
The cycle every ideal client is trapped in: build process → hire person → system works briefly → business changes → system breaks → founder re-fixes it → calls it "optimization" → repeats forever. The core problem Envisioned solves. See [[ideal-client-profile#The Core Problem The Loop]].

**Capacity Mapping™**
Strategic diagnostic that maps where founder intelligence is trapped and where it can be extracted. The X-ray before the surgery. Not a strategy doc — a structural blueprint. See [[capacity-mapping]].

**Su Misura**
Italian for "made to measure." The philosophy behind [[the-atelier]]. Nothing templated. Everything custom-fitted to the founder's methodology, not adapted from someone else's framework.

**The Bleed**
When founder's personal identity and business identity become so enmeshed that separating them feels impossible. Not a weakness — a signal that codification is urgent. The founder IS the methodology, which is exactly why it needs to be extracted.

## Content & Voice

**Story-Weave Method**
Content creation framework: Moment → Tension → Reframe → Conviction → Filter. Story first, thesis second. The reader is inside a moment before they know they're learning something. Borrowed from Suzi Gray's mechanics, voiced by Maria-Ines. See [[story-weave-method]].

**The 6 Doors**
Content rotation system ensuring variety: Ethics/Identity, Myth-Busting, Case Study/Proof, Philosophy, Positioning, Movement/Vision. Every door leads to the same room (The Codification Thesis). From Agus di Carlo's mechanics. See [[content-strategy]].

**NASAmeter**
1–10 scale for checking pretentiousness in copy. Named because "NASA doesn't need to tell you they're smart." Target zone: 3–4. Below 3 is too casual. Above 5 is performing intelligence instead of demonstrating it. See [[nasameter]].

**Diagnostic Fragment**
Sentence structure: incomplete. Used to create tension and rhythm. "Not because you're replaceable. But because your intelligence is finally scalable." The em dash, the fragment, the correction — Maria-Ines's signature syntax. See [[voice-guide]].

**Permission-Based CTA**
"DM WORD" or "Comment WORD" — never "What do you think?" or "Drop a 🙌." The CTA functions as a psychological filter. Only people who self-identify with the word will act. Everyone else scrolls. This is by design. See [[content-strategy]].

**Story-Living**
Beyond storytelling. The brand doesn't tell stories about transformation — it lives them. The grocery-line business, the solo trip, the Paris atelier. Content IS the proof. See [[brand-narrative-architecture]].

## Offers & Business

**The Atelier**
Two-tier in-person intensive in Paris. Foundation (€3,500, half-day) and Build (€5,500, full-day). Physical, bespoke, proof that business runs while you're somewhere beautiful. See [[the-atelier]].

**Codified in the City (CiTC)**
Proof-of-concept experiential offering. "I want Milan." Not a workshop — a lived demonstration that the methodology works in real time, in a real city, during real life. See [[codify-in-the-city]].

**Time-Rich CEO**
90-day full founder extraction. The signature transformation program. €7,500-10,000. Everything that makes the founder the bottleneck gets structurally eliminated. See [[time-rich-ceo]].

**Workflow Extraction**
30-day done-with-you removal of one specific founder-dependent workflow. DECODE → DESIGN → DEPLOY → MAINTAIN. Proof of concept for larger engagements. See [[workflow-extraction]].

**Genius Map**
Entry-level diagnostic (€197-297). The first time a founder sees her own methodology mapped. Not comprehensive — catalytic. Creates the "oh my god" moment.

**Perishable Genius**
What happens to founder intelligence that isn't codified. It has a shelf life. When the founder burns out, gets sick, takes a break, or scales — uncaptured genius spoils. The urgency driver.

**Bridge Language**
The principle that every offer applies forward. "Work is never wasted. It's foundation." No sunk cost between rungs. See [[offer-architecture]].
