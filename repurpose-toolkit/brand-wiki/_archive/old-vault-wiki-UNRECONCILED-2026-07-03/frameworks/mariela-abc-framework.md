# Mariela de la Mora — ABC Content & Story Selling Frameworks

> Extracted from Mariela's "Story Selling" masterclass and applied to Envisioned's content system. The MECHANICS are borrowed. The VOICE and POSITIONING are 100% Maria-Ines.

---

## The ABC Content Ratio (50/30/20)

Every piece of content serves one of three functions. The ratio determines whether your content attracts, builds trust, or converts.

### A — Attention (50%)
- Human, shareable, philosophical
- NOT selling anything
- The content that makes people stop scrolling and think
- Identity-level posts, bold reframes, personal stories
- On Substack: free essays, Notes
- On Instagram: Reels, philosophical carousels

**Maria-Ines examples:** "The Privilege of Refusing AI," "If AI Does the Work, Who Are You?", "Nobody Needs a Clone"

### B — Building (30%)
- Trust, expertise, education
- Shows process in action
- Demonstrates how your brain works
- Teaching without being a how-to
- On Substack: mix of free + paid essays
- On Instagram: educational carousels, process Reels

**Maria-Ines examples:** "I Almost Cried Connecting an MCP," "You Are Irreplaceable. Your Patterns Aren't.", "Perishable Genius"

### C — Conversion (20%)
- Direct sell, client stories, CTA
- Social proof and transformation evidence
- Offer-forward content
- On Substack: paid tier, offer announcements
- On Instagram: case studies, "DM [WORD]" posts, launch content

**Maria-Ines examples:** Codified in the City announcements, client transformation stories, Michelin Chef post (B/C hybrid)

### Application Rule
In any batch of content (weekly plan, content harvest), check the ABC ratio. If Building (B) is overindexed, add more shareable philosophical pieces (A). If Conversion (C) is missing entirely, add a client story or offer mention.

**Current Envisioned pattern (from March 2026 analysis):**
- A: 36% (under — needs more philosophical, shareable content)
- B: 21% (under — but close enough)
- C: 42% (over — too much promotional without enough trust-building first)

**Corrected target:** More A content to lead, B to bridge, C to close. Not the reverse.

## Story Selling

Mariela's core principle: **Story shortcuts the buying process.** Emotional before logical.

### The Rule
"Bring me to a moment. Give me an example. Be more specific."

This maps directly to the [[Story-Weave Method]]:
- **Moment** → "Bring me to a moment"
- **Tension** → The gap the story reveals
- **Reframe** → "Give me an example" of the deeper truth
- **Conviction** → "Be more specific" about what this means

### Three Sellable Stories
Every brand needs these three stories. They're not three posts — they're three lenses applied across ALL content:

1. **The Unmet Need You See**
   - Maria-Ines's version: Founders trapped inside their own businesses, mistaking busyness for value, unable to separate identity from labor

2. **How Your Story Lets You See/Serve It**
   - Maria-Ines's version: Venezuelan infrastructure collapse → systems thinking. Watching centralized authority fail → building decentralized, founder-independent architecture

3. **Your Unique Process/Skills**
   - Maria-Ines's version: Codification → AI infrastructure → Freedom Architecture. The methodology of extracting founder intelligence into systems that operate without them

## Lead Magnet as Pre-Sell

Mariela's reframe: A lead magnet is NOT a freebie. It's a **mini introductory offer that pre-sells your method and builds trust before the pitch.**

### Substack Application
- The free tier IS the lead magnet
- Every free essay pre-sells the worldview
- The paid tier pre-sells the methodology
- The methodology creates demand for the done-for-you offer

### Funnel Logic
```
Notes (discovery) → Free essay (philosophy) → Paid essay (architecture) → DM/reply (conversation) → High-ticket offer (transformation)
```

## Taking Attention Offline

Mariela's principle: **Don't give social media every job.**

| Platform | Job |
|----------|-----|
| Instagram / LinkedIn | Get attention → drive to Substack |
| Substack Free | Deepen trust → convert to paid |
| Substack Paid | Build relationship → convert to conversation |
| DM / Email / Reply | Convert conversation → discovery call |
| Discovery call | Convert to high-ticket offer |

Each platform has ONE job. When you try to sell on Instagram, you're asking a discovery platform to do a conversion platform's job. Attention and conversion happen in different emotional states.

## How to Use This Framework

When creating content:
1. **Check the ABC ratio** — is this batch 50/30/20?
2. **Identify which of the 3 Sellable Stories** this piece is telling through
3. **Open with a moment** — Mariela's "bring me to a moment" = [[Story-Weave Method]]'s MOMENT
4. **Assign each platform its job** — don't ask Instagram to convert. Ask it to attract.
5. **Pre-sell, don't sell** — every free piece builds demand for the next layer
