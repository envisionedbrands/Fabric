# Audience Language Bank — How They Talk About Their Problem

> The specific words, phrases, and emotional patterns the [[ideal-client-profile|ideal client]] uses. This is the raw material for content hooks, sales copy, and DM conversations. Every phrase here comes from real client language or documented audience patterns.

---

## Conscious Language (She'll Say This Out Loud)

These are phrases she uses in DMs, on calls, and in her own content:

**About the bottleneck:**
- "I need to get my systems together before I can scale"
- "Everything lives in my head"
- "My business should work without me"
- "I need infrastructure, not another person"
- "I want someone to just build this for me"

**About delegation failure:**
- "I can't delegate because I can't explain how it works"
- "Every time I hire someone, I still have to explain everything"
- "It's faster to just do it myself"
- "My team waits for me to make every decision"

**About the shame:**
- "If people saw how this actually works, they'd lose respect"
- "I'm too smart to be this disorganized"
- "My business looks great from the outside but inside it's chaos"

**About AI frustration:**
- "I've tried AI tools but nothing sticks"
- "Everyone's talking about AI but nobody's showing me how it actually works for MY business"
- "I don't need another tool — I need someone to build the system"

---

## Unconscious Language (She Feels This But May Not Say It)

These are the deeper emotional currents — use them in content to create "she's reading my mind" moments:

- "Am I doing this right?"
- "Will I ever not be the bottleneck?"
- "What would it feel like to actually rest?"
- "Is it wrong that I want both empire and ease?"
- "Can my genius be turned into something other people can operate?"
- "What happens to my business if I get sick?"
- "I've built something amazing but I'm trapped inside it"

---

## Words They Resonate With

**Positive (use frequently):**
Partnership, strategic, custom, legacy, essence, brilliance, freedom, architecture, infrastructure, codify, intentional, sovereignty, bespoke, clarity, foundation

**Negative triggers (use to name the problem):**
Bottleneck, dependency, trapped, duct tape, chaos, hustle, burnout, grind, hamster wheel, ceiling, stuck

**Words that repel them (NEVER use):**
Proven system, template, step-by-step, hack, trick, scale fast, passive income, automated funnel, 6-figure, boss babe, manifest

## Objection Language

How she frames resistance (not as objections, but as internal dialogue):

| What She Says | What She Means | What Envisioned Says Back |
|--------------|---------------|--------------------------|
| "I need to think about it" | "I'm scared this won't work for MY business" | "That's exactly the right instinct. This only works if it's built for your specific methodology." |
| "Can I do this myself?" | "I don't want to depend on another person" | "You could. But you're paying yourself $15/hour to do $500/hour thinking." |
| "What if my business is too messy?" | "I'm ashamed of what you'll find" | "That's literally what we do. The mess IS the raw material." |
| "I've been burned before" | "Someone oversold and underdelivered on AI" | "Good. That means you know what generic looks like. This isn't that." |
| "Is this just for big businesses?" | "Am I successful enough for this?" | "This is for businesses where ONE person's intelligence runs everything. That's you." |

See [[objection-architecture]] for full objection handling framework.

## Emotional States by [[buyer-journey|Buyer Journey Stage]]

**Awareness:** Frustration, self-blame, resignation ("this is just how it is")
**Consideration:** Hope mixed with skepticism ("can this be real?")
**Decision:** Quiet resolve ("I'm done waiting")
**Implementation:** Vulnerability + relief ("someone finally sees this")
**Transformation:** Liberation + advocacy ("let me tell someone about this")

## Content Application

When writing content, match the language to the door:

| [[content-strategy#The 6 Doors\|Content Door]] | Language Register |
|------|------------------|
| Ethics/Identity | Unconscious language. "What would it feel like to..." |
| Myth-Busting | Negative triggers being named and reframed |
| Case Study | Conscious language in the "before," positive words in the "after" |
| Philosophy | Neither — Maria-Ines's own framework language |
| Positioning | Contrast: their words (conscious) vs. Envisioned's words (positive) |
| Movement/Vision | Aspirational positive language |
