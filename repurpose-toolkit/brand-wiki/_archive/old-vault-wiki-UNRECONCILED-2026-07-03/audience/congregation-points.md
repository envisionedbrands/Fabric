# Congregation Points

> Where the ideal client hangs out online and offline — tiered by reach and relevance.

---

## Discovery Channels (Ranked by Conversion)

From customer journey intelligence — ranked by actual buyer conversion, not volume:

1. **Referrals / Word of mouth** — #1 source of actual buyers. Sophisticated audience trusts peer recommendations over marketing.
2. **Facebook** — Primary social platform. Where the lurkers live — established founders who observe for weeks/months before reaching out.
3. **Instagram** — Credibility checkpoint. The silent buyer pattern: never likes, never comments, then DMs "I want Milan."
4. **Substack / Newsletter** — Long-form converts differently. Philosophically aligned from day one.

## Strategic Implications

**For content distribution:**
- Facebook and Instagram are the primary platforms for reaching ICA
- Substack for depth — attracts most philosophically aligned audience
- Reddit communities for understanding language and pain points (research, not posting)

**For referral strategy:**
- The highest-quality leads come from lifestyle-visible transformations
- Clients don't say "Maria-Ines is great" — they say "look what I can do now"
- The transformation is the marketing

**For positioning:**
- These communities are full of people who've tried the Type 1-3 solutions and are frustrated
- The "I've tried AI before and it didn't work" cohort is the most pre-qualified audience
- Content in these spaces should name The Loop and failed solutions — not pitch
