# The Curly Hair Stylist — raw capture

> Captured verbatim from voice note, 2026-06-12. Do not polish. This is the source.

I went to the hair salon last week. My hair is curly, and not everybody knows how to cut curly hair. So because they are experts at what they do, I gave the reins to their process. I just told them what I wanted and let them do their thing.

The woman who styled my hair — it's the second time I've had her, but it's been like six months. I thought maybe it was a fluke last time. I never walk away from a hair salon happy a hundred percent, because they tend to treat all hair the same. She didn't put a lot of product on my hair. And I'm not saying I need a lot of product — but I definitely know how much I need.

This week she put only a little bit of cream at the ends of my hair, and a little bit of gel. Even though she asked the right questions — she asked me, do you want the hair to be hard? Which means, for curly girls, we know what we're talking about: we need a little bit of cast so the hair lasts longer. I said yes, I want it hard.

But she kept doing everything, quote-unquote, right — but not the way that I do it. And the results, not surprising, were not good for my taste. My hair was very frizzy. She kept touching it. She kept fluffing it. She kept doing all the wrong things.

So I think there's a metaphor here. We need to trust that we know what we need. We need to trust our expertise. And I come in to help them harness their expertise — not to implement my method, but first and foremost to power their expertise, not mine.

## Strategy notes (added in chat, 2026-06-12)

**On-thesis?** Yes. This is the codification thesis, not The Loop. Core differentiator: *your genius, your system* vs. everyone else's *use my framework*.

**The inversion to catch when drafting:** In the story she is the *client* and the stylist is the provider who failed her. The metaphor only flatters Envisioned if Maria-Ines is the **anti-stylist**. Keep two characters straight:

- The **founder** = the curly-haired woman who knows exactly how much product her hair needs. Hands the reins to experts who flatten that into generic method. Walks away frizzy, again, not surprised. (= the ICP feeling: my self-knowledge lives in my head, so even handed over it comes back generic.)
- **Codification** = writing down "this much cream, this much gel, yes to the cast" so the system executes *her* way every time — instead of re-explaining to a new stylist every six months and hoping.
- **Maria-Ines / Envisioned** = the stylist who refuses to fluff. Doesn't impose her cut. Extracts how *they* do it and builds the system to run it precisely.

**The sharpest line in it:** the gap between "she asked the right question" and "she did it her own way" is the entire gap between a discovery call and codified infrastructure. *Asking the right question isn't the same as honoring the answer in the execution.*

**Caution:** "trust the expert" isn't quite the lesson — the expert *was* trusted and still failed. The hero is the client's self-knowledge; Envisioned is the provider who refuses to flatten it.
