## Action Summary — For the Client

The goal we landed on: make the **contrast and payoff** of adopting AI visceral, not abstract. People grasp on the surface that AI can do a lot, but it feels like loose puzzle pieces that don't fit — they want to take the potential "by the horns" but don't know how.

- **Make the "before" concrete.** Break down what a founder actually does on a daily/weekly basis — team, operations, marketing, delivery — so it's obvious how little time is left for the work they love.
- **Show the "after" as lived experience,** not features: the leisurely lunch, picking kids up from school, space to dream about what's next, a better client experience.
- **Anchor on "codify your brain"** as the required first step — the flip only happens when AI is properly infused into the business, and that starts with codifying.
- **Reframe the offer narrative:** move from "trust my authority" to "here's exactly how I work with people — this is where they start, this is what I do, this is the result it creates."
