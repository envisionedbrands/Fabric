# BRAND COMPASS — Envisioned (María-Inés)

The strategic filter for ALL content selection and writing in this toolkit.
Source of truth: `brand-wiki/Envisioned wiki/` — core-messages.md, one-word.md, thought-leadership.md (2026-07-03, her final wording), content-pillars.md, positioning-compass (March 2026). When the wiki updates, refresh this file.

## The lens (One Word): Generic → Codified
Envisioned exists because uncodified genius comes out generic; everything we make moves it toward codified. **Every piece of content must name Generic or advance Codified — content that does neither is off-brand by definition.**

**NON-NEGOTIABLE usage rule:** "Generic" NEVER describes the woman — only the OUTPUT. The diagnosis is always structural, never personal: "You're not generic. Your output is — because your genius isn't codified yet." Any draft that lets "generic" touch a person's identity gets rewritten.

## The thesis (north star)
"AI isn't a productivity hack — it's a mirror." It forces the question of who you are when effort no longer proves worth.

## The three core messages (her verbatim wording, 2026-07-03)
1. **Two readers.** "Your brand has two readers now. Humans still look for resonance. Machines look for language they can understand, retrieve, and trust." — against "personal branding = visual identity."
2. **Presence is fragile; codification travels.** "If your brand only lives in your presence, it is fragile. Codification turns your voice, standards, methodology, and offers into infrastructure that can travel." — against "founder-led means founder-everywhere."
3. **Uncodified genius comes out generic.** "When your genius is uncodified, the system fills in the blanks with everyone else. That is why brilliant work starts sounding generic on its way out." — against "you need better prompts/tools."

## Five thought-leadership pillars (clip & post selection targets)
1. **The Generic Tell** — brilliant work turns generic on its way out (clients' drafts, delegation, the machine layer)
2. **Speak Human and Machine** — two readers; "Agents don't look at pretty. They read language." Access → Adoption → Authorship
3. **Standards in Every Room** — methodology present at the moment of client execution; amplified, never replaced
4. **The Long Lunch Is the Proof** — presence is the outcome of codification; the living is the evidence
5. **The Mirror** — identity, ethics, access; "Effort is not a virtue. Design is."

## Deployment discipline
- **Maximum ONE "isn't X. It's Y." line per published piece** — only at the true hinge, never the default hook.
- **Jargon budget: max 2 proprietary terms per piece**, each earned with a concrete example.
- **Client stories require permission**: the mentor/ChatGPT-tell story and client build-day stories are flagged in the wiki as needing the client's OK before publishing. If a clip or caption leans on a client story, flag it for approval rather than publishing language that identifies anyone.

## The 7 content pillars (what she naturally gravitates to)
AI_TRANSFORMATION (79%) · REVOLUTION (44%) · FOUNDER_CEO (43%) · COMMAND_CENTER (35%) · BRAND_STRATEGY (31%) · CONVICTION (28%) · LEGACY (24%). **The strongest content combines 2–3 pillars.** Use as a lens for selection, not a checklist.

## Competitor red flags — reject content that sounds like:
- **Type 1 Technician**: tool names, "5 ways to use AI", AI as productivity hack
- **Type 2 Brand Strategist**: all identity, no systems, no operational consequence
- **Type 3 Hybrid**: surface "AI-powered" efficiency; making the founder more efficient instead of less necessary

## Accessibility filter (the NASA problem)
- Show the life, don't name the label: "Tuesday afternoon. Laptop closed." beats any label.
- After 3 abstract sentences, the next must be grounded (a scene, a physical moment).
- ≥1 sensory anchor per piece: time of day, physical space, an action, emotion through detail.
- Dinner-party test on every line.

## Platform mechanics (from the 80-post analysis)
- Instagram captions: ~850–900 characters is the sweet spot.
- CAPS: selective, for philosophical emphasis only (IDENTITY, DESIGN, INFRASTRUCTURE, COMMAND CENTER…) — never urgency, never everything.
- CTAs: call-to-specificity DM words ("DM CODIFY if…", "Comment MIRROR if…") or none — conviction can stand alone.

## Selection checklist (clips and posts)
- Names Generic or advances Codified? · Fits ≥1 thought-leadership pillar (ideally maps to a core message)? · Combines 2–3 content pillars? · Could a Type 1/2/3 say it? (if yes, skip) · Grounded sensory moment? · ≤1 "isn't X. It's Y."? · "Generic" never touches a person? · Client story flagged for permission?
