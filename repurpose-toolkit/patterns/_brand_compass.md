# BRAND COMPASS — Envisioned (María-Inés)

The strategic filter for ALL content selection and writing in this toolkit. Source: Positioning Compass (Envisioned Brands, March 2026) + Positioning Statement.

## Position
Envisioned is the **Type 4 Freedom Architect** — the only brand at the intersection of AI systems architecture + strategic business design + founder freedom philosophy. Core claim: AI isn't a productivity hack — it's a mirror that forces you to decide who you are when effort no longer proves worth.

## The Three Pillars (every piece must touch ≥ 2)
1. **Identity Codification** — extracting and encoding founder intelligence into systems
2. **AI Infrastructure** — operational layers that run without the founder
3. **Founder-Dependency Elimination** — the founder as author, not engine

Rule: content touching only one pillar risks sounding like a competitor type. The best content connects two and arrives at the third by implication.

## Red flags — reject content that sounds like:
- **Type 1 Technician**: leading with tool names, "5 ways to use AI", how-to without philosophy, AI as productivity hack
- **Type 2 Brand Strategist**: all identity no systems, "find your authentic voice" with no operational consequence
- **Type 3 Hybrid**: surface "AI-powered content", making the founder more efficient instead of less necessary

Sounds like Envisioned when: the diagnosis lands at structure (not effort), identity work connects to systems that run without the founder, AI is infrastructure, the outcome is freedom through design.

## Accessibility filter
- **Jargon budget: max 2 proprietary terms per piece** (Freedom Architect, Intentional CEO, Identity Codification, etc.) — each earned with a concrete example.
- **Show the life, don't name the label**: "Tuesday afternoon. Laptop closed. Your business just onboarded a client while you were at lunch with your daughter." beats any label.
- **Altitude check**: after 3 abstract sentences, the next must be grounded (a scene, a physical moment).
- **Sensory anchor** (≥1 per piece): time of day, physical space, an action, emotion through detail.

## Content structure (Heck Yes framework)
1. Resonance hook (their words, their reality) → 2. Reveal the gap → 3. Paint the transformation (specific, sensory) → 4. Close with invitation or inevitability.

## Selection checklist (for clips and posts)
- Touches ≥ 2 pillars? · Could a Type 1/2/3 say it? (if yes, skip) · Grounded sensory moment present? · Dinner-party language? · Moves the viewer from one understanding to another?
