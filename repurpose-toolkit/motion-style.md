# Motion Graphics Style — Clean & Airy (Envisioned)

The visual language for all motion graphics on María-Inés's videos: story cards, text overlays, captions, and animated inserts. Used by `/repurpose-studio` step 4 and any hyperframes-helper render. The goal in one line: **calm confidence — nothing moves fast, nothing shouts, everything breathes.**

## Design tokens (from the Envisioned system)
- **Serif for meaning:** Cormorant Garamond (500–600) for headlines and card text
- **Sans for utility:** Inter (300–400) for small labels, timestamps, captions
- **Palette:** sage `#516b62` (primary), soft cream `#faf9f6`, ink `#26312d`, muted gold `#b08d57` (accents only). Never saturated colors, never pure black or pure white.
- **Glass cards:** translucent white/cream panel, subtle blur behind, thin sage left border, generous corner radius (16–20px)

## The "airy" rules
1. **Whitespace is the design.** Cards occupy ≤ 60% of frame width; text never touches edges; padding is generous (≥ 32px). One idea per card — if it needs two sentences, it's two cards.
2. **Slow, eased motion.** Everything enters/exits with gentle ease-out over 500–800ms. Fades and 20–40px drifts only. NO bounces, spins, zooms, shakes, or pop effects.
3. **Nothing competes with her face.** Overlays sit in the upper or lower third; the speaker is the subject, graphics are annotation. CTA cards at ~190px from top (Instagram UI covers the bottom).
4. **Type animates by line, not by letter.** Lines fade/rise in sequence (100–150ms stagger). Karaoke word-highlight is reserved for captions only — sage highlight, subtle scale (≤1.06).
5. **Thin rules and underlines as motion.** A 1–2px sage line drawing itself under a key phrase is the preferred "emphasis" animation. Understatement over decoration.
6. **Hold time:** every card stays readable for at least 1.5× the time it takes to read aloud. When in doubt, longer and calmer.
7. **Max 1 graphic element on screen at a time.** Airy means empty. If two cards would overlap in time, cut one.

## Card types
- **Stat card:** one number large (Cormorant, 64–80px), one-line label beneath (Inter, small caps)
- **Process card:** 2–4 short steps, revealed line by line
- **Quote card:** her sentence verbatim, sage left border, no quotation-mark decoration
- **CTA card:** one line + one DM word ("DM CODIFY"), gold accent allowed

## Reference calibration — DONE (2026-07-08, from 4 reel frames of instagram.com/instacoachandy)
What María-Inés responds to in this style, translated to Envisioned tokens:

1. **Pill cards, two-level hierarchy.** Main statement on a white rounded-rectangle pill (bold, generous padding, soft shadow), with a smaller cream sub-pill beneath for the parenthetical/qualifier. Keyword inside the main pill emphasized by COLOR (our sage `#516b62` or gold `#b08d57`), not by animation. Adopt this as the default text-overlay shape.
2. **Acronym / framework letter tiles.** Stacked rounded-square tiles (cream `#faf9f6` fill, sage letter) spelling a framework letter by letter, revealed sequentially (150ms stagger), each with its definition card sliding in beside it. This is the signature move for teaching her frameworks (MAP, CITC, the Three A's).
3. **Device mockups as B-roll.** Floating phone frames showing example content over the talking head, annotated with dashed-line highlight boxes around the part being discussed. Use for "look at this example" beats.
4. **Emoji as one comedic beat.** A single emoji (occasionally repeated ×3 for one beat) as punctuation in a promo/personal moment — then gone. Educational/diagnostic overlays stay emoji-free per brand rules.
5. **The airiness is the canvas.** Neutral background, overlays never covering the face, one element at a time, generous negative space. Cards are crisp and high-contrast; the calm comes from the space around them.

Keep Envisioned's type system (Cormorant for meaning, Inter for utility) and palette — we adopt her card LANGUAGE, not her fonts/colors.
