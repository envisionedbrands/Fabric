# Motion Graphics Style — Clean & Airy (Envisioned)

The visual language for all motion graphics on María-Inés's videos: story cards, text overlays, captions, and animated inserts. Used by `/repurpose-studio` step 4 and any hyperframes-helper render. The goal in one line: **calm confidence — nothing moves fast, nothing shouts, everything breathes.**

## Design tokens (from the Envisioned system)
- **Serif for meaning:** Cormorant Garamond (500–600) for headlines and card text
- **Sans for utility:** Inter (300–400) for small labels, timestamps, captions
- **Palette (authoritative — from `brand-wiki/Envisioned wiki/identity/visual-design-system.md`):**
  - Canvas `#FBFAF9` (cool off-white — NEVER warm cream) · card fills `#F7F5F0` · stone `#EDE9E3` for section fills · borders `#E3DED5`
  - Ink `#1E1E1E` text (never pure black)
  - **Olive `#4C5A2E` — PRIMARY accent** (kickers, rules, CTAs, keyword emphasis). Olive-deep `#3A4622` for emphasis.
  - **Sage `#8A9A6B` — tertiary accent** (dashes, dots, closing flourishes)
  - **Rose/terracotta `#B87A5D` — emotional accent, used sparingly** (personal-story moments, warm beats)
  - Taupe `#8A7A68` for metadata/captions
  - **EXCLUDED: gold (the wiki says "never gold") and burgundy/maroon. Never saturated colors.** Proportion rule: accents stay thin — if olive is hitting 15%+ of a frame, pull back.
- **The signature card frame (from the reference feed — uniform across everything):** white/cream fill with a **thin ink outline (1.5–2px) drawn just OUTSIDE the fill, slightly offset** (2–4px down-right), rounded corners (10–14px), no heavy drop shadow. This offset-outline treatment is the default frame for every pill, tile, and card — it's what makes the feed read as one system.
- **Glass cards:** reserved for overlays directly on footage where a solid card would block too much (translucent cream, subtle blur, thin sage left border)

## The "airy" rules
1. **Whitespace is the design.** Cards occupy ≤ 60% of frame width; text never touches edges; padding is generous (≥ 32px). One idea per card — if it needs two sentences, it's two cards.
2. **Slow, eased motion.** Everything enters/exits with gentle ease-out over 500–800ms. Fades and 20–40px drifts only. NO bounces, spins, zooms, shakes, or pop effects.
3. **Nothing competes with her face.** Overlays sit in the upper or lower third; the speaker is the subject, graphics are annotation. CTA cards at ~190px from top (Instagram UI covers the bottom).
4. **Type animates by line, not by letter.** Lines fade/rise in sequence (100–150ms stagger). Karaoke word-highlight is reserved for captions only — sage highlight, subtle scale (≤1.06).
5. **Thin rules and underlines as motion.** A 1–2px sage line drawing itself under a key phrase is the preferred "emphasis" animation. Understatement over decoration.
6. **Hold time:** every card stays readable for at least 1.5× the time it takes to read aloud. When in doubt, longer and calmer.
7. **Max 1 graphic element on screen at a time.** Airy means empty. If two cards would overlap in time, cut one.

## WHEN to use motion graphics (the intelligence layer)
Graphics are placed by listening to the content, not by decoration. Analyze the transcript/beat structure first, then place graphics ONLY at these beat types:

| Beat in the video | Graphic |
|---|---|
| The hook (first 1–3s) | Hook text in the signature outlined pill, always |
| A framework or steps named | Letter tiles / process card, revealed as she names each part |
| A specific number or result | Stat card, appears exactly when the number is spoken |
| A punchline or quotable sentence | Quote card OR thin sage underline drawing under the phrase — pick one |
| "Look at this example" | Device mockup with dashed annotation box |
| The CTA | CTA card with DM word, ~190px from top |
| **Emotional or personal story peaks** | **NOTHING. Cut all graphics — her face carries it.** |

Density rules: at most one graphic per 8–15 seconds; never two on screen at once; if a beat qualifies for two treatments, choose the quieter one. A 45s clip typically earns 3–5 graphic moments including hook and CTA — if the plan has more, cut the weakest.

## Card types
- **Stat card:** one number large (Cormorant, 64–80px), one-line label beneath (Inter, small caps)
- **Process card:** 2–4 short steps, revealed line by line
- **Quote card:** her sentence verbatim, sage left border, no quotation-mark decoration
- **CTA card:** one line + one DM word ("DM CODIFY"), olive accent on the DM word

## Reference calibration — DONE (2026-07-08, from 4 reel frames of instagram.com/instacoachandy)
What María-Inés responds to in this style, translated to Envisioned tokens:

1. **Pill cards, two-level hierarchy.** Main statement on an off-white rounded pill (`#FBFAF9`, bold, generous padding, the signature offset ink outline), with a smaller `#F7F5F0` sub-pill beneath for the parenthetical/qualifier. Keyword inside the main pill emphasized by COLOR — olive `#4C5A2E` by default, rose `#B87A5D` on warm/personal beats — never by animation. This is the default text-overlay shape.
2. **Acronym / framework letter tiles.** Stacked rounded-square tiles (`#F7F5F0` fill, olive letter, offset ink outline) spelling a framework letter by letter, revealed sequentially (150ms stagger), each with its definition card sliding in beside it. The signature move for teaching her frameworks (MAP, CITC, the Three A's).
3. **Device mockups as B-roll.** Floating phone frames showing example content over the talking head, annotated with dashed-line highlight boxes around the part being discussed. Use for "look at this example" beats.
4. **Emoji as one comedic beat.** A single emoji (occasionally repeated ×3 for one beat) as punctuation in a promo/personal moment — then gone. Educational/diagnostic overlays stay emoji-free per brand rules.
5. **The airiness is the canvas.** Neutral background, overlays never covering the face, one element at a time, generous negative space. Cards are crisp and high-contrast; the calm comes from the space around them.

Keep Envisioned's type system (Cormorant for meaning, Inter for utility) and palette — we adopt her card LANGUAGE, not her fonts/colors.
